# SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE: a fictional appeal decision, not a real case

Carrow v. Delmont Holdings Ltd., 2031 XXCA 412

Reasons for Judgment of the Court

[1] The appellant appeals the order of a chambers judge dismissing her application to extend the time to file a notice of claim.

[12] The appellant submits that an extension should be granted whenever the applicant shows a bona fide intention to proceed, without regard to any prejudice to the respondent.

[14] The chambers judge held that, on an application to extend time, the applicant must show (a) a bona fide intention to proceed formed within the period, (b) a reasonable explanation for the delay, and (c) that the respondent would suffer no prejudice that cannot be compensated. In so holding, the judge cited and followed Abbott v. Birch, 2019 XXCA 101 at para. 30, where this Court set out those three requirements.

[15] The judge found that the appellant had not shown a reasonable explanation for the delay, and dismissed the application.

[22] I see no error in the judge's statement of the test. She identified the correct requirements and applied them. The appellant's submission that prejudice is irrelevant cannot succeed: it is contrary to Abbott.

[23] Nor do I see any palpable and overriding error in her finding on the explanation for the delay.

[24] I would dismiss the appeal.
