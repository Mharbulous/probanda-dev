# Form 2 — Response to Civil Claim

The Housekeeping code for a Response to Civil Claim is `RNOCC`.

This skill currently has no registered Form 2 template, fill configuration, or
Form 2 drafting rules. Do not infer formatting from another response form and
do not treat this file as Form 2 implementation. Until Form 2 is added through
the missing-template workflow, classification must continue to report the
missing template and no Form 2 style guide is loaded for drafting.
