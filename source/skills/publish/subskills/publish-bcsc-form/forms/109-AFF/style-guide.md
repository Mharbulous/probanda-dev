# Form 109 — Affidavit

Read this guide only when the classified form is an affidavit (`AFF`).

## Not for affidavits of service — with one exception

An affidavit proving service has its own prescribed form: Form 15 for personal
service (`subskills/publish-bcsc-form/forms/015-AFS/style-guide.md`), Form 16 for ordinary service, and
Form 16 modified for service under a Rule 4-4 order
(`subskills/publish-bcsc-form/forms/016-AOS/style-guide.md`, substituted service variant). Do not draft any
of them on this template.

**Service under a Rule 4-4 order is not the exception.** Rule 4-6(1) prescribes
Form 15 only for personal service (the mode defined by R. 4-3(2)) and Form 16
only for ordinary service on a party who has provided an address for service
(R. 4-2(2)), so neither form fits substituted service as written: Form 15's
para. 2 deposes to "handing it to and leaving it with" the person, and Form
16's four branches are each tied to an address for service, a fax number, or an
e-mail address in that character. R. 4-6(4) preserves "any other evidence of
service that the court considers appropriate", which would permit a Form 109.

That was formerly drafted on this template and flagged ambiguous. **It is
resolved (practitioner decision, 2026-08-05):** such an affidavit is drafted on
**Form 16, modified to match the terms of the order** under R. 22-3(1), which
directs that a prescribed form be used "with the variations that the
circumstances of the proceeding require" — the variation being that section 2
mirrors the order's own subparagraph hierarchy instead of electing one of four
check-boxes. Research on 2026-08-05 located no judicial authority deciding the
point either way; the resolution rests on R. 22-3(1), not on a holding. The
classifier no longer routes Rule 4-4 service proof here, and no longer flags it
ambiguous.

**The exception that remains.** Form 109 is correct where the affidavit must
depose to facts **beyond proving service** — for instance a combined narrative
of service attempts supporting a further application, or an affidavit needing a
Rule 22-2(7) interpreter endorsement. When drafting such an affidavit here:

- Depose to the terms of the entered Rule 4-4 order, the acts done in
  compliance with each of its terms, and the date and time of each act.
- Rule 4-4(2) requires a copy of the entered order to be served **with** the
  document, unless the court otherwise orders or the permitted method is
  advertisement (in which case the advertisement is in Form 10, R. 4-4(3)).
  Depose to that service and exhibit the order.
- Drop the top-right identifier block: R. 22-2(3) requires it on every
  affidavit *other than an affidavit of service*. Leave `affidavit_ordinal`
  blank and delete the block in Word. Rule 22-2(10) likewise excepts an
  affidavit of service from sequential exhibit page numbering.

Apply `subskills/publish-bcsc-form/forms/000-SOP/style-guide.md`. The same confirmed deponent name,
swear-or-affirm choice, sworn-or-affirmed label, and affidavit date must be
used consistently wherever each value repeats in the header, body, jurat, or
interpreter section. Ask for `affidavit_ordinal`; never infer it.

## Content drafting

1. **No section headings.** Use numbered body paragraphs only. Do not insert
   heading paragraphs such as “The Promissory Note” or “Payments Received.”
2. **Standard preamble.** The first numbered paragraph must be:

   > “I am the [role] in this action and as such I have direct knowledge of the facts herein deposed, except where stated to be based on information and belief, in which case I verily believe those facts to be true.”

3. **Facts only—no argument.** Each paragraph recounts a fact, exhibit, or
   observation within the deponent's knowledge. Omit legal argument,
   characterizations of another party's conduct, and legal conclusions.
4. **Bold exhibit references.** Every occurrence of `Exhibit [Letter]` in a
   body paragraph is bold, for example: “Attached hereto and marked as
   **Exhibit A** is a true copy of…”.
5. **Sequential exhibit lettering.** Assign A, B, C, and so on in the order
   exhibits first appear. Do not skip letters or order them by importance.
   **Letters restart at A in every affidavit** — a deponent's second affidavit
   begins again at A, never at the letter after their first affidavit's last.
   Because they restart, a citation reaching outside the affidavit it sits in
   must carry the affidavit number: `Exhibit A to Affidavit #2 of <name>`. The
   R. 22-2(8) certificate does not cure that ambiguity — it identifies the
   affidavit by deponent and swearing date only, never by the R. 22-2(3)(b)
   sequential number, so two affidavits by one deponent sworn on the same day
   carry certificates that read identically.

   *Footing.* This is the **firm's convention**, not a requirement of the
   Rules. Rule 22-2 and Rule 8-1, read complete from the consolidation,
   impose nothing on exhibit labelling across affidavits or across rounds;
   letters running on would breach no provision. What the Rules do say is
   narrower, and none of it reaches the label: R. 22-2(8) prescribes the
   marking certificate but leaves the blank after "Exhibit" unconstrained, so
   letters and numbers both comply, and it scopes each exhibit to one
   affidavit; R. 22-2(10) sequences exhibit *pages* within one affidavit,
   counting pages of an exhibit held back under R. 22-2(9)(b) or (c) and not
   attached; and the word "exhibit" does not appear in Rule 8-1 at all. Never
   state the restart as a requirement of the Rules or of registry practice.

   The court's published practice directions and administrative notices were
   audited on 2026-09-20. The index was captured in full (Cumulative Index,
   stated current to 2026-07-06), and the 16 instruments **in force** that bear
   or might bear on affidavit exhibits were read verbatim end to end. **None
   prescribes exhibit lettering** — not whether exhibits are lettered or
   numbered, not whether letters restart at A in each affidavit, and not
   whether they run on across affidavits or across successive rounds of
   applications.

   What the instruments in force do say, none of it reaching the label:
   **PD-75** (*Chambers Practice*, eff. 2026-07-06) para 11(c) restates the
   exhibit *page*-numbering requirement, sequential from the first page of the
   first exhibit to the last page of the last, per R. 22-2(10); **PD-72**
   (*Electronic Application Records*, eff. 2026-03-31) Appendix A requires
   every document and exhibit in an electronic application record to be
   bookmarked, using "Exhibit A: BMO Bank Statements 2023-2025" only as an
   illustrative bookmark title; **PD-73** (*Virtual Chambers*, eff.
   2026-03-31) Appendices A and C reproduce the standard form instruction to
   identify each affidavit as "Affidavit #[sequential number, if any, recorded
   in the top right hand corner of the affidavit]", which is the R. 22-2(3)
   block. **PD-1** (*Affidavits — Identification of Counsel or Commissioner*)
   was rescinded 2024-09-09, its PDF has been withdrawn from the court's site,
   and it was not read; it is not part of the law in force.

   Three limits travel with that finding and must be repeated wherever it is
   relied on. First, **the 16 were selected by title** from a 129-entry index,
   so an instrument whose title gave no indication was not read. Second, **the
   court's index omits at least one rescission** effected by an instrument's
   own text: it still lists AN-19 as current although PD-69 para 1 expressly
   rescinds it, so index status is a finding aid, not a guarantee. Third,
   **unwritten registry counter practice is not addressed by this audit** and
   remains unaudited: no published source speaks to it, and nothing here
   claims anything about it either way.

## Template post-processing

6. **Interpreter endorsement off by default.** Remove the Rule 22-2(7)
   interpreter endorsement unless `interpreter_required` is affirmative.
7. **No “last updated” footer text.** Clear text runs from every Word footer.
8. **No footer page numbers.** Footer field-code runs are cleared with the
   other footer runs.
9. **Keep one body paragraph with the jurat.** Apply `keepNext` to the last
   numbered paragraph before the jurat table so at least one numbered
   paragraph appears on the signature page.

Repository enforcement: `subskills/publish-bcsc-form/scripts/fill_plain.py` performs rules 6–9.

## Template provenance

`subskills/publish-bcsc-form/templates/affidavit.dotx` is maintained by hand. It
was converted once from a LEAP export by
`subskills/publish-bcsc-form/scripts/convert.py` (`--form affidavit`, 96d9e7d /
4c8fc4c), and every later change was made to the converted file itself.
`convert.py` unwraps content controls into placeholders, repairs runs and
numbering, and strips vendor parts, but never rewrites body text, so running it
on a LEAP export again would lose every edit below. In particular it would
restore LEAP's original jurat wording, which the Rule 22-2(5) layout replaced
and the affidavit assembly step rejects. Edit the shipped template in place; do
not regenerate it from LEAP.
