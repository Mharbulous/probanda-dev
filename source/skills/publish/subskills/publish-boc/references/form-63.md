## Layout contract

- `Form 63 (Rule 14-1 (20) )` sits above the style of proceeding.
- `DEFAULT JUDGMENT BILL OF COSTS` is centered and bold, with the Rule 22-3 line in italics
  directly beneath it. The source shows no other line between the style of proceeding and the
  title.
- The "bill of costs of" line and the "Amount involved" line are centered, above the table.
- COSTS, DISBURSEMENTS and TOTAL ALLOWED are one five-column table: description, a label column
  that holds `Total:` and `Applicable taxes:` right-aligned, Claimed, Allowed, and an unheaded
  fifth column.
- The fifth column is filled on three rows only: each `Total:` row and `TOTAL ALLOWED`.
- The Allowed column, the fifth column, the date and the signature belong to the assessing
  officer.
- `COSTS`, `DISBURSEMENTS` and `TOTAL ALLOWED` are bold. `Total:` and `Applicable taxes:` are not.
