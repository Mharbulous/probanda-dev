# Audience: the public, and the person it concerns

Read when: the letter goes to a public body or a non-party, or may otherwise be shared beyond its recipient.

- **Every letter to a public body may be disclosed.** A public body's file can be produced to the person complained about, or released on an access request. Write nothing you would not want that person to read.
- **Assume a letter to a non-party will be shared.** A non-party may pass it to the other side, to its own lawyer, or to the public.
- **Disclose no more than the request needs.** Privileged or personal information goes in only where the request says the client has instructed it.
