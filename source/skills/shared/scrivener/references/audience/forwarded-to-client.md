# Audience: the other side's client

Read when: the letter goes to a lawyer who may forward it to their own client, which is every letter to opposing counsel.

- **Assume the letter may be shown to the client, the court, or both.** Write nothing about the other side, or its lawyer, that you would not want that client to read.
- **Address counsel, not their client.** Use "you" and "your client", and never write past counsel to the client.
- **No commentary on counsel to their client.** A remark about counsel's conduct or competence, read by their client, hardens positions on both sides and earns nothing.
