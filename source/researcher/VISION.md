# research — Vision

`researcher` answers what the law is. It is the one plugin that states law: every statement comes from the `research-agent` agent through `/legal-research`, with a pinpoint citation to an audited authority. It also holds the read side of the shared legal-commons pool.

It requires `legal` and names nothing in `litigator`, except to say that a step is another skill's job.

The vision, the design principles and the anti-patterns that govern all five Probanda plugins are in the `VISION.md` of the `legal` plugin. They apply here unchanged.
