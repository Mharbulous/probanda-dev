# lawyer — Vision

`lawyer` holds skills that any lawyer can use, whatever their practice area or jurisdiction: `dyu` restates a request and waits for confirmation before acting, and `TLDR` summarizes a session for someone who has read none of it.

It requires no other plugin, and no other plugin names it.

The vision, the design principles and the anti-patterns that govern the Probanda plugins are in the `VISION.md` of the `legal` plugin. They apply here unchanged.
