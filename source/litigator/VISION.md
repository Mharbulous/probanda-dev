# litigator — Vision

`litigator` does the court work on a matter: it applies the law that `research` returns to the matter's facts, drafts filings and correspondence as mockups, fills court forms, binds filing packages and calculates interest and costs.

It requires `legal` and `research`. It keeps no data store of its own: its skills use the `data` server that `legal` ships.

The vision, the design principles and the anti-patterns that govern all five Probanda plugins are in the `VISION.md` of the `legal` plugin. They apply here unchanged.
