## Section 14: How a reply at the Opening routes

- A Ready job named: `references/job-session.md` Branch A. In a session that has created jobs, or has completed, released or shelved a job, only a job it created in this session takes Branch A, and only while it holds no job. Any other Ready job named there is refused in one line: this session may be assigned only a job it created in this session, and a new `/draft` session takes this one (`references/dispatch.md` section 14, Dispatch). An In Progress job named, with no steps or at least one step still open: `references/take-over.md`. Naming a job does not assign it to this session. An in-progress job is always another session's at Opening, whatever token it carries, because nothing has been assigned to this session yet. Its lock lapses after 60 minutes idle, and the board then shows it in Ready (ADR 0015). Beyond naming its idle window, in no reply, at the Opening or later, say or imply that another session's lock is live, abandoned or ended. Never show a holder's identity token or invent a name for it.
- An In Progress job named whose card shows every step resolved (☑, or ☒ or ⏸ on the practitioner's words, `/drafting-data` §6b): one decision form carrying the job's title, its steps (all resolved), read off its card (`board_card(jobs: [N])`), and the line "Every step is done. Close it?", or "Every step is done or set aside. Close it?" when any is ☒ or ⏸, with the options "Close it" and "Leave it", both also named in the framing prose, and no skip button. When the job's `keys:` line shows any key still deriving (`(now: <glyph> "...")`), the form also carries `/drafting-data` §9's line "its marker still fires on N of M items", read off the same card, so the one yes answers §9's still-firing question too. **Close it:** run `drafting_data__assign_job` with `takeover: true`, `expected_holder` and `expected_last_activity`, the `holder:` tag and the `last activity:` minute off the same card. A `lost_race` result stops as `references/take-over.md`'s **Take over** says: stop and ask, never re-run to win. A `holder_changed` result stops as `references/take-over.md` says for it. Then run `/drafting-data` §9 in full: the queued-partner supersession and the token-guarded completion in one script, the §9 confirm-read, then §14's gate job check and a re-render. The session is then assigned no other job it did not create (`references/dispatch.md` section 14, Dispatch). **Leave it:** write nothing and carry on with the Opening. The form is reached only by the practitioner naming the job, never offered.
- Asked which job to take over: one line, theirs to choose. Name or describe no job. Write nothing.
- A quick edit asked for, a reworded paragraph or a move, recast, removal, restoration or kill of a paragraph, or a reorder of material to be relied on ("change fact 3 to ...", "move paragraph 8 to after paragraph 14", "remove paragraph 15", "reorder Part 4"): `draft/references/revise-on-the-fly.md`, not the outlining interview.
- A reply to a message from the tribunal, a registry or another party's counsel, asked for as a reply and not as a letter ("reply to the arbitrator's email"): `draft/references/reply.md`. It is not a Writing and takes no job, so nothing is outlined or assigned.
- New work: Branch B, the outlining interview.
- Loose threads to rank, review or triage: `references/loose-thread-triage.md`. To work a thread, the practitioner names the Work Board it goes on, and it is outlined there. It is an outlining session: a thread outlined there is a job this session created, under Dispatch's rule for such a session (`references/dispatch.md` section 14).
- Proposals all held by one job: that job named. Otherwise name each holder in one line, found by `board_card(key: …)` for each key, outline those in Proposed.
- A Proposal named ("approve the Smith note-up"): its approval, found on `board_card(lanes: ["proposed"])`. Intake writes nothing at an Opening, so every Proposal waits in Proposed until it is named or pulled. Branch B, entering at that pick: each Proposal named unedited is written straight to Ready as its own job, before any interview question. Nothing is asked, then render. A held key is named to its holder instead (above), a shelved key is named as shelved and restorable on request, never restored implicitly, and an edited or merged pick is outlined there. Say in one line that the session can take one of the jobs it just created when the practitioner names it, and no other (`references/dispatch.md` section 14, Dispatch).
- A group or filter of Proposals named ("all the exhibit pinpoints", "everything for Submission 3"), or a task typed with the invocation that names a submission: the intake (`references/opening.md`, Intake), which writes them by `/drafting-data` §3e. It is not outlining. A named batch then waits; a typed task routes on as typed once its submission's Proposals are written.
- "Take the next" (or "what next?") in a session with no job: SKILL.md section 14, Dispatch. With Ready empty, intake first writes one job's worth from Proposed.
- A Blocked job named whose blocker is superseded or shelved: Branch B, entering at that job rather than at its opening question.
- A job or Proposal shelved or restored: apply it as bookkeeping (below), then carry on with the Opening. It is not an answer to the opening line. A typed `restore job #N` first takes `/drafting-data` §10a's state read, because a deleted job is on no card: a deleted job is undeleted, a shelved one unshelved, and a job that is neither is reported in one line with nothing written. A restored job whose work is research and that is not in the form goes to the migration review before any of its work (`shared/research-job-migration.md`). A restore refused because another job now holds its key is reported as `/drafting-data` §10a words it.
- A job deleted ("delete job #N", a job that should never have been written): bookkeeping, like a shelving, applied by `/drafting-data` §10a's delete, then re-render. A Ready job, or the session's own in-progress job. Another session's in-progress job is refused. The job stays in the drafting database and shows on the Work Board page only as a struck red line in Completed, with a Restore link that copies `restore job #N` for the practitioner to paste here. Received, never offered: where the work was merely not wanted, shelving is the answer.
- A job brought forward ("hold job #N until 2 November", "BF job #N to the first business day of the week after next", "clear the BF date on job #N"): bookkeeping, like a shelving, under the `bring_forward` rules (`skill_rules__applicable`), applied by `/drafting-data` §10b's write, then re-render. A calendar date is written as the practitioner gave it. A calendar phrase is resolved by §10b's read-only query, read back as a plain date, and written only on their yes. Only a queued, unshelved job. The date is the day the work may start, never a deadline (`shared/deadline-isolation.md`). Received, never offered: never propose or infer a BF date, never work one out except from the practitioner's own phrase by §10b's query, and never offer to change or clear one. A Bring Forward job named for work is answered in one line with its date; clearing the date is the practitioner's to ask for.
- A job released: a release asked for on a job in progress in another session takes the take-over form (`references/take-over.md`), whose "Return it to Ready" is that release. Say so in one line, and write nothing until the form is answered. The job can be shelved instead.
- A court-ready Word document or PDF asked for (for signature, for filing) while a drafting criterion is unmet: `draft/references/assemble-then-gate.md`, "The practitioner's override". Publish it now, with the unmet criteria on its cover page, never a question first and never a refusal.
- The Writing is finished: `draft/references/retire-filing.md`. Nothing is assigned, nothing is outlined. Never on a retired Writing.
- The Writing was filed: record the date by `/drafting-data`'s lifecycle write and carry on. A general Writing's Sent date is recorded only by the Sent write, `draft/references/letters.md` "Sending".
- The registry rejected the filing: the re-file is the same Writing, never a new one, and a rejection is not a lifecycle state (ADR 0023 D5). If the filing date was recorded, clear it by `/drafting-data`'s undo idiom. A notice of application or application response recorded as served keeps its `filed_on`, the service date: a rejection at set-down or under 8-1 (14.7) never clears it. Outline the rejection's reasons as jobs in the practitioner's words (Branch B). The registry's package number and the rejection date go into the wording of the Writing's to-do, through `/task-prioritization`.
- A view page asked for by name: SKILL.md section 11, View pages. It takes no job and writes nothing.
- A to-do asked for: hand it to `/task-prioritization` as one outcome pointing to the open Writing (ADR 0023 D2). Never create one here, and never a to-do for work toward this Writing: that work is a job on this board.
- Completion criteria or subject changed: a decision form, `Retire, open new` (`retire-filing.md`, then section 8 in the next turn) or `Change a job only` (a job named, above).
- A different Writing: opened directly when named, otherwise the Writing picker (section 7).
