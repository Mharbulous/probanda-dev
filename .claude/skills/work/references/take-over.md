# Taking over a job in progress

An in-progress job is always another session's at Opening, nothing has been assigned to this session yet, and a matching token does not make it yours. Read its card (`board_card(jobs: [N])`). The practitioner's instruction to take over the named job is the yes, whether outright or on a stated condition the session can check and has just confirmed on that card ("if it is still idle"): run **Take over** with no form, and say "Taking over does not stop the other session." in the announcement. Never in an unattended run. Otherwise, before any write, render a decision form: the job's title, what its card shows (when it was taken, its last activity, its steps and which are done, the loose threads captured from it, the answers saved for it and its sources to get, with no fabricated identity; the `holder:` tag is passed to the tool, never shown), the line "Taking over does not stop the other session. Returning it to Ready does not stop it either.", and the question "This job is in progress in another session. Take it over?" with options "Take over", "Return it to Ready" and "Pick a different job." No skip button; all three options also stated in the framing prose. Nothing else goes in the form. Anything else noticed is captured, and raised in the announcement.

<!-- atom id=work.take-over.choices ops=take_over -->
- **Pick a different job:** write nothing, and wait for the practitioner to name another job from the board.
- **Take over:** run `drafting_data__assign_job` with `takeover: true`, `expected_holder` and `expected_last_activity`, the `holder:` tag and the `last activity:` minute off the card the decision was made on. The write lands only while the job still shows both. `assigned`: add the job's started entry to `ProceduralHistory.md` with `matter_data__procedural_history_append`, dated today (`shared/procedural-history.md`), and continue. `lost_race`: a third session won; stop and ask, never re-run to win. `holder_changed`: nothing was written; re-render, tell the practitioner the job changed hands or moved since the card was read, and show the decision form on the fresh card, even after an instruction; never auto-retry. `recently_active`: nothing was written; say in one line that a job active in the last 30 minutes is never taken over, and wait (ADR 0015). `no_match`: the board was stale and the job is already completed, superseded or shelved, or released back to Ready, which the result's `status` and `rejected_at` show; surface that, re-render, and go back to Opening.
- **Return it to Ready:** run `drafting_data__release_job` with `expected_holder`, the `holder:` tag off the card the form was built from, only after the practitioner picks it. `released`: re-render, say in one line that the job is back in Ready with its plan and done steps kept, dispatch nothing this turn, and wait. `taken_over`: nothing was written; re-render, tell the practitioner the job changed hands since the card was read, and show the decision form again on the fresh card; never auto-retry. `shelved`, `completed` or `no_match`: nothing was written; say which, re-render, and go back to Opening. The option exists only on this form, which is reached only by the practitioner naming the job and is never shown in an unattended run. Nothing about it says whether the other session is live (CoClerk ADR 0037, amended by ADR 0015).
