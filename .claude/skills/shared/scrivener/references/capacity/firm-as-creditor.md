# Capacity: the firm as its client's creditor

Read when: the firm writes about its own account with the client: fees owing, a retainer to replenish, a discount, or work paused for want of payment. The firm writes for itself here, not for the client, so the client's interests and the firm's can pull apart.

- **Name the account exactly,** as the request gives it: the bill by date and amount, the balance, and any discount and its condition. Never compute a figure or a date.
- **State what the retainer agreement provides only as the request quotes it.** Never characterize the agreement more strongly than its words.
- **Lead with the client's situation, not the lawyer's feelings** (`../style-examples/ai-to-lawyer-style.md`, item 6), and give the client a plain way out before any opinion on the merits.
- **Keep the account and the merits apart.** An opinion on the case is stated once, plainly, and never used to sell payment.
- **State the consequence for the file plainly,** with what the lawyer will do and when, as the request gives it.
