## Branch A: a job is chosen

### Plan

<!-- atom id=draft.premise.holds ops=plan_readback,take_over when=premise:holds -->
| What the subtraction leaves | What the practitioner is told |
|---|---|
| Nothing, every key still derives | One line in the header: each item's marker still fires. The work proceeds (Announce and proceed, below), unless a step only restates the request (`work/references/job-session.md`). |
<!-- atom id=draft.premise.failed ops=plan_readback,take_over when=premise:failed -->
| One or more keys | Name those items in your own visible reply, then take the Change path. Never proceed as stated. |
<!-- atom id=draft.premise.none ops=plan_readback,take_over when=premise:none -->
| No keys at all | Outlined by hand; no marker premise to check. |
<!-- atom id=draft.premise.mixed ops=plan_readback,take_over -->
| Keys, plus work described by hand | Report the keyed part and name the rest as unchecked. |

<!-- atom id=draft.premise.failed-path ops=plan_readback,change when=premise:failed -->
**The failed path is a scoped Change, never a refusal.** A grouped intake job with some keys still live takes its own rule instead (below). Name the moved items, ask one question in the outlining interview's manner ("with these out, how should the steps read?"), record it verbatim, read it back once, and write it per `/drafting-data` §5. Where the session words it from the record instead, it needs no adoption (`shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3). One turn whether one key moved or twenty. That same write clears the dead keys from the job's conversion record: the practitioner has just dropped that work, and a marker firing on that row again must not stay hidden behind this job. The check never refuses the job, never releases the lock, and never sends the job to a fresh outline. It is one of the two stops before execution (Announce and proceed, below), never a second pause. A one-step job is checked before it is assigned instead, by the pre-claim check (`work/references/job-session.md`).

<!-- atom id=draft.steps ops=plan_readback when=job_steps:many -->
**Steps:** the steps written with the job at outlining are its plan, as recorded and as the pickup review (`work/references/job-session.md`) left them before the job was assigned. Once it is assigned they are never re-divided or reworded. The one exception is the plan upgrade (`shared/job-planning.md` section 3), which rewrites only the steps not yet done. Where the practitioner asks for different steps, that is the Change path below. There, one action, or one action repeated over a set the approved wording names ("pull and read three follow-up cases"), is one step, and actions that differ in kind or depend on each other's result ("audit the three memos; record the verified propositions with pinpoints; re-render") are separate steps. A division the session words is drawn from the record and needs no adoption (`shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3).

<!-- atom id=draft.one-step ops=pre_claim,take_over -->
**A newly assigned one-step job** runs on its creation approval once its step names work. No pause: the pickup review, the restating check and the pre-claim check (`work/references/job-session.md`) have already run. Its one step, written at creation, or the steps the pickup review split it into, is the plan, and is never the plan marker (`work/references/job-session.md`, "A job that needs a plan"). It takes the header below (Announce and proceed), 0 steps done, then dispatches. An intake job's creation is the intake itself (`work/references/dispatch.md` section 14).

<!-- atom id=draft.note-up ops=note_up -->
**An intake note-up job on a statute, regulation or rule** keeps the steps intake wrote, one per provision the Writing cites (`/drafting-data` §3e). Its steps are its plan, never re-divided or replaced by the recorded intent: a single step runs as the one-step job above with that step unchanged, and two or more are announced as written. One written with no step asks the practitioner for its steps before it proceeds and proposes none. The job runs the currency check through `/legal-research` once, at its first step, naming every step's provision, and the gate relays the enactment's one legislation record (`shared/legal-research.md`, "Statute currency."). Every later step reuses that record and checks nothing again, and so does any other job on the same enactment in this session (the record's `key`). Intake writes the note-ups of one Act as one job, `Note up: <Act>`, holding every key that names the Act and one step per provision (`/drafting-data` §3e, "One title, one job"), so that one check serves them all. Such a job records the record's `path` and `currency` on every authority its keys name, not only the first. Each step quotes its provision by pinpoint from the file at the record's `path`, never from a summary, a memo or a CanLII call. It records through `/drafting-data` the record's `path` as the authority's locator, its `currency` as the authority's currency result, and its `currency_marker` as that provision's "current to" date. A record whose `path` is `none` leaves the provision unquoted, and the step says so. A grouped intake job (`/drafting-data` §3e), one step per Proposal holding its item, keeps its steps the same way: they are its plan, never re-divided, and are announced as written. On the premise check a dead key drops that key's step and keeps the others as written. That is a Change under `/drafting-data` §5 with no wording question, because every step that stays is record text. When every key is dead, no step would stay, so it takes the failed path above unchanged. A job whose steps were split along its item's own numbered or lettered parts (`/drafting-data`'s `filing-checklists.md` §6a) keeps its steps the same way, as its plan, never re-divided. Its one key dead means every step goes, so it takes the failed path above too.

<!-- atom id=draft.announce ops=pre_claim,plan_readback,take_over -->
**Announce and proceed.** Every job this session works that is not completed, newly assigned, taken over or restored, one step or many, is announced and then worked without asking. This is the standing rule in place of a plan approval. Once the premise check and the restating check (`work/references/job-session.md`) have passed, print this header in your own visible reply, never only inside widget markup or text between tool calls, which some runtimes summarize away. In such a host (Cowork), put it in the reply's final text or send it through the host's direct user-message tool (e.g. `SendUserMessage`), never both:

1. **Which job:** `Job <N>`, by number, never the `# Job <N>: <title>` heading again, which is printed once, when the job is assigned (SKILL.md, Job header). A stock bundle job (`/drafting-data` §3b), whose fixed intent says "this letter", "this memo" or "this Writing" and names neither, adds its title and the Writing it sits on, by the title the board prints for that Writing and its type: `Deliver, for <Writing title> (Unstructured Writing)`. The type is `letter` for a letter kind, `memo` for an `internal-memo`, `Unstructured Writing` for an `unstructured` or `unstructured-<recipient>` Writing, and the filing kind for a court filing's stock job. Then the recorded intent quoted verbatim, adding "outlined in an earlier session, not re-checked since" when it was outlined in an earlier session, and the premise check's one line. Where the pickup review retitled the job, add `Retitled from: "<old title>"`.
2. **Progress so far:** `<d> of <m> steps done`, listing each step done in an earlier session in its recorded words, or "none yet". Where the pickup review split a step, add `Split from: "<old step>"` for each. Where the job's card prints open sources to get, list them on a line of their own, `Sources still to get: <labels>`, apart from the steps: they are the job's record, never part of the plan.
3. **Planned this session:** each step not yet done, in its recorded words, as legal work ("audit the three memos; record the verified propositions with pinpoints; re-render"), never as tool calls or database operations, with no substance added.
4. **Order:** those steps as a numbered list in the order they will run.

Then print exactly `Proceeding with the planned work now.` and, before the first step runs, stamp the approval with `drafting_data__approve_plan` (`/drafting-data` §5). On an inherited plan it returns `already_approved` and writes nothing, so the inherited stamps survive. Then execute end to end, stamping each step as it completes, until the job is complete. An unattended run prints no header and dispatches as `references/unattended-run.md` says.

The only stops before the first step are a dead key (the failed path above) and a step that only restates the request (`work/references/job-session.md`). A reworded item is reported in the header and does not stop. Once work runs, the only stops are:

- work the session cannot do itself: an obstacle the plan did not anticipate (An obstacle on any assigned job, below), or human-only work, which is listed for the practitioner (Movers, below);
- a one-way-door act: recording a Writing filed or sent, retirement, taking over or superseding another session's job, a deletion, merging a duplicate job, or waiving a pre-filing check;
- a hook refusal, quoted verbatim, then stop (SKILL.md, the no-blocking-checkpoint rule);
- anything deadline isolation forbids (SKILL.md, deadline isolation);
- a statement of law, which goes only through `/legal-research`.

A two-way-door choice, one a later write can undo, proceeds without asking.

<!-- atom id=draft.readback.change ops=change -->
- **Change**, when the practitioner asks for one or a stop above requires it: one question at a time, in the outlining interview's shape (`work/references/job-session.md`, Branch B), recorded in their words, read back once, approved, executed. Same job, same session. Nothing new is assigned and nothing new is outlined. A re-approval replaces the full step list and re-stamps the approval. A change needs no adoption ceremony, whoever words it (`shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3). A changed plan meets `shared/job-planning.md`.

<!-- atom id=draft.obstacle ops=obstacle -->
**An obstacle on any assigned job**, one-step, taken-over or restored included, is a permitted interruption once its work runs (Announce and proceed, above, lists the others): something the plan did not anticipate. First read the matter folder and case data on it. Ask the practitioner only what those leave open. State it in one line of prose in your reply, never a list or step. Then offer only change, a scope expansion (letting the job edit more) if the work needs material outside its scope, or spin off blocker jobs if the obstacle is separate work this job must wait on, such as a research question or a document to obtain. Never abandon or defer. Say only what each does to the job: no example, fact, figure or blocker title. Content follows the choice (`shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3). In an unattended run nothing is offered: it reports the obstacle and stops (`references/unattended-run.md`).

<!-- atom id=draft.obstacle.change ops=obstacle,change when=obstacle:in_job -->
- **Change:** the Change path above. On a one-step job it replaces the full step list and re-stamps the approval, as for any job (`/drafting-data` §5). As there, a change the practitioner words themselves needs no ceremony, and one the session proposes takes it. On a taken-over or restored job, say first that the inherited stamps will be discarded. Where the change hands a step to the practitioner, the re-approved step is in their words and lists as theirs under Movers (below). The job stays open and its marker keeps firing.

<!-- atom id=draft.execute ops=execute -->
### Executing the plan as a worklist

The markers on the mockup are the worklist. There is no second task list to build or drift out of sync with the document.

<!-- atom id=draft.execute.threads ops=execute -->
- **Loose threads before research:** before researching anything for the job, check the `## Loose Threads` lines of `board_card(lanes: ["loose_threads"])` for ideas on the job's topic. They are every open loose thread in the proceeding, and one captured on a sibling Writing names that Writing in its closing parenthesis (`from Job #N on <Writing title>`, or `from <Writing title>`). Reading a loose thread does not consume it: only a later outlining session does, or the job whose intent is working the loose threads, which consumes the thread it worked.
<!-- atom id=draft.execute.loose-threads-job ops=execute,plan_readback,pre_claim,route_reply when=job_held:true -->
- **The loose-threads job:** when the held job's recorded intent is working the loose threads, read `work/references/loose-thread-triage.md`, "Working the threads without a pick", and follow it. The threads are the job's work: it asks no pick question, and an instruction about how it does that work, a bare "continue" included, is applied and never captured as a loose thread.
<!-- atom id=draft.execute.lock ops=execute -->
- **Lock check before each research dispatch:** immediately before every `researcher:research-paralegal` dispatch and every law-audit agent spawn the job makes, through `/legal-research` or otherwise, run `/drafting-data`'s lock read before a dispatch on the job. Only this session's own token on it dispatches. Anything else dispatches nothing: stop and report what the row shows, naming any answer already saved under the job. A job taken over in another session is no longer this session's to research, and no write guard stops a dispatch.
<!-- atom id=draft.execute.sources ops=execute -->
- **Sources to get:** before a dispatch that retrieves a decision or an enactment, and before extracting or ingesting a file, record each one on the job with `drafting_data__track_source`, and close it once it has landed (`/drafting-data`'s `jobs-common.md` §2, "Sources to get"). That call is the lock check for the dispatch. A source is the job's own record and never a step: the plan and its step count stay as approved, and no Change is taken to add one. Its label is a citation, an instrument or a file name, never what an authority holds and never a deadline.
<!-- atom id=draft.execute.save ops=execute -->
- **Saving an answer under the job:** save each research answer the job receives with `research_filing__save_answer`, passing the job's `db_path`, `ticket_id` and this session's `session_token`, so the job's card lists it and its idle clock restarts. A result reading `job.recorded: false` means the job is no longer this session's: the file is saved, so report it and stop.
<!-- atom id=draft.execute.flags ops=execute -->
- **matter-data flags in a research return:** read each `matter-data:` line in the return's notes (`shared/legal-audit-protocol.md` Step 6), in the same turn the return arrives. For each `correction` or `discrepancy` flag, first read the source its evidence ids name: where SKILL.md rule 9 covers the flag, correct the record and give rule 9's report in place of the relay below, and capture nothing. For every other flag, capture comes first (SKILL.md section 14, "Captured ideas"), by the duplicate test in `shared/legal-research.md` ("After the answer"): every `pinpoint` flag together as one idea, and each other flag as its own. Each flag is relayed in one line with the offer to record it through `/matter-data`, and the job carries on. No blocker is proposed unasked: a flag becomes a blocker only when the practitioner says this job must wait on it, and never a flag that restates the held job's own subject, which the duplicate test names in one line instead. Then take the Spin off path above for that flag. Its two values are fixed frames, `<kind>` being the flag's kind and `<flag>` its description and evidence ids, character for character: title `Resolve the matter-data <kind>: <flag>`, intent `What should case data record? <flag>`. Put both in the visible reply as plain text, each whole on one line, and say they come from the research return. The rest is the Spin off path unchanged. Whichever session is later assigned the blocker records the answer through `/matter-data`, and the session states no law. A return with no `matter-data:` line changes nothing.

<!-- atom id=draft.execute.markers ops=execute -->
- **Reading markers:** a clean line means the component is done, nothing prints for done. Our own outstanding work renders after the component it belongs to; events caused by the other side or the world render as a prefix in front of the work they routed. Applying "what work is left" to an event marker is a category error.
<!-- atom id=draft.execute.movers ops=execute -->
- **Movers:** every marker maps to a mover. Retrievable work goes to subagents. Law goes through `references/authorities.md`. Evidence, witness statements and admissions go through `references/evidence.md`. Human-only work (a deponent swearing, a witness signing) is listed for the practitioner and never dispatched or silently dropped. A statement whose scrutiny failed has its review job as its next step, and that job's decision is the practitioner's, never a subagent's, and the statement is never listed as needing fresh scrutiny (`references/authorities.md` Failed scrutiny). The `Prepare the review pages` job runs by `references/counsel-review.md`. The `Link or draft the factual basis` job first asks whether the test is on file: `/drafting-data`'s read of the submission's legal basis (its scrutiny reference, "Read a submission's legal basis"), on the submission the job's key names, run as the job's first act and before any fact is shown. The test is on file when that read shows at least one statement verified or settled. When it does not, say so in one line as the obstacle, then take the Spin off path above without being asked, proposing one research blocker: title `Research the test: <item label>`, intent `What must be shown to establish this contention? <item label>`, `<item label>` being the item label (`/drafting-data`'s `filing-checklists.md` §6a) built from the key's table and id and the item on the job's `keys:` line. Its one step carries the contention in full: the intent's frame followed by that item character for character (`/drafting-data` §3f), so the contention is stored once, in the step. The two frames are fixed and the label and the contention are the record's own words, so the session states no law, and which rules or tests apply is `/legal-research`'s to answer, never split out here. Put both values in the visible reply as plain text, each whole on one line, say they come from the job's Proposal item, and the rest is the Spin off path unchanged. With the test on file, the job opens with a proposed set of facts already on file to link to the contention, each with its source, a one-line reason it bears on the contention, and any fact on file that cuts against it, and asks the practitioner to approve or change the set (`shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3, propose from the record). No link is written before their yes. Where no fact on file supports the contention, the job says so as a gap and never fills it with a fact the record does not hold. It closes when facts are linked, or when counsel states in their own words why the contention needs no stated fact (recorded by `/drafting-data`), a reason the record cannot supply, which the skill asks for and never proposes. The `Link or draft the proposition it requires` job is its law-side mirror: it proposes a proposition already on file to link, with its one-line reason, and writes no link before the practitioner's yes; with none on file it names the obstacle in one line and spins off the same `Research the test: <item label>` blocker with the same two frames and the same step; it closes when a proposition is linked, or when counsel states in their own words why the contention needs no stated law (recorded by `/drafting-data`), which the skill asks for and never proposes; and while it stands on the same submission, the factual-basis job's test-on-file step names the proposition job as the obstacle and spins off nothing, so one piece of law work is never routed twice. A letter is drafted as a Writing, saved first (`references/procedure.md` section 8) with its stock jobs, by `references/letters.md`. Open days the letter offers as hearing dates come from `/practice-data` court-calendar-days, fetched by this session. The `Availability letter` job's letter is BCSC's `letter` kind, saved on the proceeding of the job's own Writing, and its sending is the Sent write of the `Deliver` job that save writes. A stock `Availability letter` job's plan is always the one step `/drafting-data` §3b gives now, whatever intent and steps its record carries.
<!-- atom id=draft.execute.rhythm ops=execute -->
- **One at a time:** the practitioner says what is done about each marker, one at a time, in their own words. The list that runs is theirs, read back.
- **Fan-out:** how the list fans out is a mechanical choice stated in one line and made by the skill. Independent items run in parallel, items touching the same passage run in turn, an item waiting on another runs once that answer lands. Only the main session spawns: a subagent has no Agent tool, so a step that needs an agent is never handed to one.
- **Reporting:** short summaries, interrupt only when stuck.
- **Regeneration:** one regeneration at the end of a dispatched batch, revision bumped, presented as a file card (`shared/present-files.md`). Markers whose work finished are gone; human-only markers remain until the practitioner clears them.

<!-- atom id=draft.opposing ops=opposing_points -->
### Opposing points

Where a point attacks is asked, never inferred, offered as the Writing's own fixed list of submissions and orders. An opposing point is captured the moment it is forwarded, whatever the session was about. Where an answer leaves part of a point unmet, ask about that part, never compose the rest (facts the matter already holds may be offered for the practitioner to pick from). Deliberate silence is recorded, not implied: a point held for the hearing, or declined outright, is a decision on the record. The finish line is zero unrecorded rebuttals, not zero unanswered points.

<!-- atom id=draft.complete ops=complete -->
### Complete

Before completing a job converted from Proposals, re-render and check which of its keys still derive on its card (`board_card(jobs: [N])`), by `/drafting-data` §9: while any does, name it in one line ("its marker still fires on N of M items") and complete only on the practitioner's yes, otherwise the job stays in progress; an unattended run reports and stops. A job with a source to get still open is completed the same way: named in one line, and only on the practitioner's yes (`/drafting-data`'s `jobs-common.md` §2, "Before completing a job with open sources"). A research job whose last research return carried a `gap:` line naming a tier not run is not completed: name the gap in one line and run that tier by `shared/legal-research.md` ("On a held job, an open tier is not an answer") first, unless the practitioner says in their own words to complete the job without it: then set each of that tier's steps ☒, or ⏸ when they say to put it off, with `drafting_data__set_step`, passing their words verbatim, before completing; an unattended run reports and stops. Before completing a research job, also compare the saved memo's headings with the job's title (`shared/research-memo-template.md`, "A job's memo"): one `## Issue` holding the title verbatim, a `### Sub-issue` for each part of a title with more than one question mark, each the part verbatim and in order, and no other issue or topic heading. On any difference, name it in one line and re-invoke the same agent to rewrite the memo to the job question, then save it again; the job is not completed until they match, unless the practitioner says in their own words to complete it as is; an unattended run reports and stops. Completion is the `drafting_data__complete_job` call, which also rewrites the Milestone, so no separate render follows it. It refuses with `steps_open` while any step is ☐, ↻ or ⏳, naming each (`/drafting-data` §9): finish those steps, or set one ☒ or ⏸ only on the practitioner's own words, never to clear the refusal. After it succeeds, add the job's completed entry to `ProceduralHistory.md` with `matter_data__procedural_history_append`, dated today (`shared/procedural-history.md`). When done, the session is assigned no other job it did not create: it may be assigned a Ready job it created in this session when the practitioner names it, one held at a time (`work/references/dispatch.md` section 14, Dispatch). An unfinished job stays in progress under this session's lock and surfaces for take-over next session, unless the practitioner tells the session to release or shelve it (`references/shelve-and-restore.md`), after which the same holds.
