Read when: retirement chosen, the Writing is said to be finished, or a retired Writing is offered for recovery, or the practitioner asks to close or reopen a Work Board with no Writing.
Assumes SKILL.md is loaded. Rules are not restated here.

**Framing.** Retirement is an elicitation, not a checkpoint (SKILL.md's no-blocking-checkpoint rule). It asks what only the practitioner knows, then writes, with nothing inside it pausing for approval. Retirement and recovery sit outside the job regime, because a retirement ends a Writing's jobs and no job can scope either. Run the steps in order and stop at the first that refuses. Filed and waiting refuses before step 1 (SKILL.md prohibition 10): record the filing date instead. A notice of application adjourned generally is not retired either: it stays live, and `/prepare` offers `Reset for hearing` (Rule 8-1 (21.1)). Say so and stop. **The refusal read then runs, still before step 1:** a job on this Writing held in progress by another session is never superseded silently. For each such job, show its take-over decision form (`work/references/take-over.md`) and act on the answer as that file says, with two changes for this route. "Return it to Ready" that returns `released` goes on to the next such job, then to step 1, in the same turn. "Take over" that returns `assigned` is followed at once by releasing the job this session now holds (`drafting_data__release_job`), because the retirement write refuses any job in progress (`/drafting-data` `jobs-closeout.md` §9a), and then goes on the same way. "Pick a different job", or any other result, stops the retirement with nothing more written. Never take a job over, clear its lock or supersede it without the form's answer, and never shelve it as a way through. **Queued jobs the practitioner wants kept** are moved to another board first, by `/drafting-data` `jobs-closeout.md` §10c, because retirement supersedes them and recovery does not undo that. The same holds before closing a Work Board with no Writing.

1. **Elicit the reason, only where the board shows work started.** First read the Writing's `filings.needs_forum_parts` through the gatekeeper, and its Work Board through `drafting_forms__board_card` with the lanes `goals`, `criteria`, `live` and `completed`. A Filing here is a Writing with `needs_forum_parts = 1`: a court or tribunal Writing, a venue's letter included, which alone can carry a decision date. A letter, memo or release with `needs_forum_parts = 0` is not one. A statement of goals exists when the goals lane reads `confirmed` or `inferred`. No per-goal achievement is recorded, so the board's completion criteria are what says whether its goals were achieved. Ask for the reason only if (a) the Writing is a Filing, or (b) the board has a statement of goals or at least one completed job, and at least one of these holds: a goal is not achieved (a criterion on the criteria lane is unmet or pending), a job is Ready or In Progress on the live lane, or a completion criterion is not met. Otherwise ask nothing and infer nothing: step 5 writes the reason `'other'`, steps 2 and 3 do not run, since a Writing that is not a Filing holds no decision, and say in one line that no reason was asked because the board shows no work started. Where the reason is asked: one decision form offering exactly the closed set of reasons the gatekeeper's checklist lists, read at the time of asking and never from memory, nothing added. Phrase each in the practitioner's own language: the appeal period ran out, appeals are exhausted, it was decided and that is the end of it, it settled, it was abandoned, it was adjourned generally, it was delivered and asks for nothing, something else. Nothing preselected, even when the practitioner already named a reason. Recording a Writing as sent never leads to this form and never offers retirement (ADR 0042): a sent Writing stays on its board until the practitioner asks to retire it, for any reason on the list.
2. **Elicit the decision, if there was one.** Offer what the Writing's dates or a `/matter-data` order show, to confirm or correct. Ask the rest: whether the Writing was decided, on what date, and the outcome. "Nothing was ever pronounced" is a complete answer. A Writing that settled or was abandoned has no decision, and inventing one is fabrication. A sent Writing that asked for nothing has no decision. The decision date rides in step 5's write, not a separate one.
3. **Where the decision was oral, elicit the treatments.** For each authority the Writing deployed and each opposing authority it answered, ask which of exactly four things the court did: followed it, distinguished it, declined to follow it, or did not consider it. Those four are the whole vocabulary, never a fifth, never a paraphrase, never a guess. Where the practitioner does not recall, leave that authority out rather than recording "did not consider" for "I cannot remember." Take the court, file number, presiding judge and date of the oral decision in the same pass. Nothing else crosses: no text of the reasons, no counsel's notes, no style of cause.
4. **Move or detach each attached draft** (ADR 0042). Read through the gatekeeper the drafts attached to this Writing's board, client letters and replies. For each one, ask the practitioner, one decision at a time, which other board of the matter it moves to, naming each live Writing's board and each pre-filing proceeding's, or whether it is detached, which leaves its files where they are. Nothing preselected, and never move or detach one on the session's own choice. Write each answer by the gatekeeper's move or detach write before step 5. With none attached, say nothing and go on. The gatekeeper refuses the retirement while one is left.
5. **Write the retirement.** First capture the work ideas the Writing leaves undone, by `drafting-data/references/jobs-run.md` §8's sweep. Then one write, composed against the gatekeeper's lifecycle checklist and its job supersession rule: retirement date and reason, decision date where step 2 produced one, supersession of this Writing's own open jobs. The script refuses too: every statement in the write is guarded, so a `wip` job appearing between the refusal read and the write refuses the retirement, and a draft still attached to the board refuses it as well (step 4).
6. **Assemble the Writing's research.** Read back through the gatekeeper, composed against its live shape: the assertions the Writing argued, the statements of law beneath them with pinpoints and stored excerpts, the authorities those rest on with verification and treatment as recorded, and the opposing authorities the Writing answered. Exclude what the practitioner already rejected, and exclude any dead-ended assertion, this step's job, not the receiving skill's. Where the practitioner ties an opposing authority to one of the Writing's own assertions, carry that tie; where they do not, carry the authority untied rather than inferring one. Shape it as the payload `/matter-data` documents for this hand-off and write it to a file under `<matter-data folder>\draft\` (`/work`'s section 6) before the next step, so a failure there costs nothing already elicited. With no research to carry, write no file, skip step 7 and say in one line that there was none.
7. **Hand the research to `/matter-data`.** Invoke it with that payload file and let its own routing choose the operation, never name one. Relay what it reports back in the practitioner's own terms and unshortened: counts, anything held back, anything escalated for human judgment, any collision with law already on file. If it has no operation for this hand-off (an older install), say so in one line and carry on, the retirement is already written and stands on its own.
8. **Re-render, board only.** Run the render naming only the Work Board. This is the one sanctioned narrowing: the Writing's other mockups are frozen as they stood at retirement and re-rendering them is refused by design, so nothing goes stale by being skipped. This render passes no appeal deadline. Its summary's State now reads Retired, and the Writing's Work Board page is moved to TRASH on the next page write; only live Writings have one. A retired Writing derives no Proposals, so its Proposed lane is empty and any direct shelving standing on it lies dormant, showing nowhere until the Writing is recovered. If it still reads a live state, the retirement did not land — a `wip` job appeared between the refusal read and the write, and the script's own guard refused it — so run the refusal read again and report its rows. The gatekeeper's retirement checklist prescribes a confirm-read on `filings.retired_on` for the same miss.
9. **Then end the turn.** After a goal change, first say the board shown is the retired Writing's.

**A Work Board with no Writing** is closed, not retired, and only when the practitioner asks. The refusal read runs as above. Steps 1 to 3, 6 and 7 do not run: it has no decision and no research, and the gatekeeper's close of such a board fixes the reason. Step 4 runs for any draft attached to it, then step 5 is that close, which supersedes the board's queued jobs. Step 8's render then reads Closed, its page is moved to TRASH on the next page write, and the Litigation Strategy page lists it as closed with the date. Step 9's consolidation is not initiated, because no Writing was retired. Reopening it is the Recovery write below, with no billing offer.

## Recovery

Recovery clears exactly what the gatekeeper's checklist says recovery clears, and nothing else, one ordinary substance write. Everything else stays as it was: the Writing's own dates, its mockups, and above all its superseded jobs, which are not un-superseded (they described the Writing as it then stood; a recovered Writing is outlined afresh). Recovery initiates nothing, and a recovered Writing later retired again is a fresh retirement that initiates once more on its own.

One offer, made once, in one line: the noting-up time recorded against the retirement was recorded non-billable, and a recovered Writing may mean the client gets the benefit of that work after all. Offer the flip and perform it only on an explicit yes, through `practice_data__set_time_entries_billable`, addressed by the batch tag the retirement's own time entry already carries, read off that entry rather than composed, its origin is outside this skill. Never flip it as part of recovery, never as a default, and never on this skill's own reading of whether the client benefited. That is billing judgment, and it is the practitioner's.
