## Section 14: Tangents

<!-- atom id=draft.tangents ops=tangent -->
**Tangents.** Up to three consecutive read-only out-of-scope turns the practitioner started (holding no job and not outlining, any except showing a mockup). On the third, offer a separate outlining session, or to expand scope (job held) or outline here (none). A tangent the skill itself notices is captured as an idea, not pursued, so it never becomes a tangent. Capturing an idea never counts as a tangent turn, and neither does a shelve, restore or release request nor the "which one?" form that disambiguates it, nor an on-the-fly revision or reorder (`references/revise-on-the-fly.md`), which is a write.
