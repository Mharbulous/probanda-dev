Read when: the practitioner names a job in progress in another session, or a taken-over, restored or released job is announced.
Assumes SKILL.md is loaded.

<!-- atom id=draft.take-over.readback ops=take_over -->
**A taken-over job:** first, once `assigned` returns and before the Job header and the announcement, run the title spelling cleanup (`/work`'s `references/job-session.md`, "Title spelling cleanup on a held job"): the header prints the corrected title, and the announcement adds `Retitled from: "<old title>"`. Then run the duplicate check (`/work`'s `references/job-session.md`, "The duplicate check"), which says nothing when it finds none. Do not take the Change path unasked: an altered re-approval recreates the step list and discards the inherited progress marks (a plan confirmed unaltered writes nothing, `/drafting-data` §5). Announce it and proceed (`references/job-session.md`, Announce and proceed): the header's progress and steps come off its card (`board_card(jobs: [N])`), and below them name the answers saved for it, the loose threads captured from it and each source to get still open, off the same card, so the work already on file is read rather than redone. Open sources are reconciled against the download manifest before any is dispatched (`/drafting-data`'s `jobs-common.md` §2, "A job taken over or restored with open sources"). Never ask whether the next unfinished step is still right. Where an unfinished step only restates the request (`/work`'s `references/job-session.md`, "A step that only restates the request"), name it as naming no work and rewrite it by that rule, and the job does not proceed until it is rewritten. Where its only step is the plan marker (`/work`'s `references/job-session.md`, "A job that needs a plan"), it is not announced as a plan: run `/job-planning` on it, whose partial replace replaces the marker, then announce the planned steps. Then compare the unfinished steps with `shared/job-planning.md` and upgrade a plan that falls short by its section 3, which keeps every done step and its stamp. If every step is done, say so and offer completion (`references/job-session.md`, Complete) or Change, starting nothing else, even work already asked for. If the job has no steps yet, ask the practitioner for them and propose none (the Change path); it is then announced and proceeds. The premise check (`/work`'s `references/job-session.md`, Plan) runs here too, but read-only: it never forces the Change path, so the inherited stamps survive. A dead key still stops the job before its next step: name the items, and ask whether the unfinished steps stand as written or change. Whenever Change is offered or asked for, say those stamps will be discarded before it is chosen. A job not yet approved is stamped approved by the announcement, its steps as they stand. A counsel's review or scrutiny review job keeps its own rule. On a taken-over `Review scrutiny finding: ` job, the take-over decision form's turn (the decision form, above) is the whole announcement: the form already shows the plan and the steps done, off the card. Nothing is added to it, and a job with no plan and no step done is not asked for steps either. After "Take over" returns `assigned`, the review turn is the `/review-proposition` template only.

<!-- atom id=draft.take-over.restored ops=take_over,restore,release -->
**A restored or released job with steps already done** takes this same title spelling cleanup first, the duplicate check, and this same taken-over announcement, for the same reason: it comes back queued with no lock, and its stamps are there to inherit. A released job also keeps its approved steps, which are announced as a taken-over job's are.
