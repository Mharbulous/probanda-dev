# Taking over a job in progress

An in-progress job is always another session's at Opening, nothing has been assigned to this session yet, and a matching token does not make it yours. Before any write, render a decision form: the job's title, what its card shows (`board_card(jobs: [N])`: when it was taken, its last activity, its `session active` stamp when the card has one, its steps and which are done, the loose threads captured from it, the answers saved for it and its sources to get, with no fabricated identity; the `holder:` tag is passed to the tool, never shown), the line "Taking over does not stop the other session. Returning it to Ready does not stop it either.", then, only when the card's `session active` stamp is within 10 minutes of the board's Rendered minute, the line "The other session last ended a turn on this job <n> min ago." (a card with no stamp adds no line), and the question "This job is in progress in another session. Take it over?" with options "Take over", "Return it to Ready" and "Pick a different job." No skip button; all three options also stated in the framing prose. Nothing else goes in the form. Anything else noticed is captured, and raised in the announcement.

<!-- atom id=draft.take-over.choices ops=take_over -->
- **Pick a different job:** write nothing, and wait for the practitioner to name another job from the board.
- **Take over:** run `drafting_data__assign_job` with `takeover: true` and `expected_holder`, the `holder:` tag off the card the form was built from, only after the yes. `assigned`: continue. `lost_race`: a third session won; stop and ask, never re-run to win. `holder_changed`: nothing was written; re-render, tell the practitioner the job changed hands since the card was read, and show the decision form again on the fresh card; never auto-retry. `no_match`: the board was stale and the job is already completed, superseded or shelved, or released back to Ready, which the result's `status` and `rejected_at` show; surface that, re-render, and go back to Opening.
- **Return it to Ready:** run `drafting_data__release_job` with `expected_holder`, the `holder:` tag off the card the form was built from, only after the practitioner picks it. `released`: re-render, say in one line that the job is back in Ready with its plan and done steps kept, dispatch nothing this turn, and wait. `taken_over`: nothing was written; re-render, tell the practitioner the job changed hands since the card was read, and show the decision form again on the fresh card; never auto-retry. `shelved`, `completed` or `no_match`: nothing was written; say which, re-render, and go back to Opening. The option exists only on this form, which is reached only by the practitioner naming the job and is never shown in an unattended run. Nothing about it says whether the other session is live (ADR 0037).

<!-- atom id=draft.take-over.readback ops=take_over -->
**A taken-over job:** first, once `assigned` returns and before the Job header and the announcement, run the title spelling cleanup (`references/job-session.md`, "Title spelling cleanup on a held job"): the header prints the corrected title, and the announcement adds `Retitled from: "<old title>"`. Do not take the Change path unasked: an altered re-approval recreates the step list and discards the inherited progress marks (a plan confirmed unaltered writes nothing, `/drafting-data` §5). Announce it and proceed (`references/job-session.md`, Announce and proceed): the header's progress and steps come off its card (`board_card(jobs: [N])`), and below them name the answers saved for it, the loose threads captured from it and each source to get still open, off the same card, so the work already on file is read rather than redone. Open sources are reconciled against the download manifest before any is dispatched (`/drafting-data`'s `jobs-common.md` §2, "A job taken over or restored with open sources"). Never ask whether the next unfinished step is still right. Where an unfinished step only restates the request (`references/job-session.md`, "A step that only restates the request"), name it as naming no work and propose how it should read by that rule's adoption ceremony, and the job does not proceed until it is adopted. Then compare the unfinished steps with `shared/job-planning.md` and upgrade a plan that falls short by its section 3, which keeps every done step and its stamp. If every step is done, say so and offer completion (`references/job-session.md`, Complete) or Change, starting nothing else, even work already asked for. If the job has no steps yet, ask the practitioner for them and propose none (the Change path); it is then announced and proceeds. The premise check (`references/job-session.md`, Plan) runs here too, but read-only: it never forces the Change path, so the inherited stamps survive. A dead key still stops the job before its next step: name the items, and ask whether the unfinished steps stand as written or change. Whenever Change is offered or asked for, say those stamps will be discarded before it is chosen. A job not yet approved is stamped approved by the announcement, its steps as they stand. A counsel's review or scrutiny review job keeps its own rule. On a taken-over `Review scrutiny finding: ` job, the take-over decision form's turn (the decision form, above) is the whole announcement: the form already shows the plan and the steps done, off the card. Nothing is added to it, and a job with no plan and no step done is not asked for steps either. After "Take over" returns `assigned`, the review turn is the `/review-proposition` template only.

<!-- atom id=draft.take-over.restored ops=take_over,restore,release -->
**A restored or released job with steps already done** takes this same title spelling cleanup first and this same taken-over announcement, for the same reason: it comes back queued with no lock, and its stamps are there to inherit. A released job also keeps its approved steps, which are announced as a taken-over job's are.
