## Section 7: The Writing picker, retired Writings and a proceeding with no Writing

**The Writing picker, only on request:** when the practitioner asks to work on a different Writing, or when every Writing is retired. A single-select card form, in session entry's order. Each card's value is the Writing's human title exactly as the session-entry call reported it, never an internal identifier. Each live card's subtitle is its mockup status from session entry: "mockups ready" when fresh, otherwise "mockups rendering…". A rendering card is answerable at once. Ask which Writing only. Never ask what to do with it. If the request names a Writing and the name identifies only one Writing, skip the picker: a live Writing opens directly, and a retired one goes straight to the recovery offer below. The picker renders when no Writing is named, or when the name fits none or several.

**Retired Writings** show no rendered paths, only lifecycle state, retirement date and reason, in a collapsed group at the foot of the picker, never mixed among live Writings, never preselected. Subtitle is the date plus the reason. Selecting one offers recovery, never a render: state it is retired and its mockups are frozen as they stood at retirement, then offer recover or pick another. The tool refuses a render of a retired Writing by design.

**A proceeding with no Writing** (a pre-filing proceeding, ADR 0018: pre-litigation work, often starting with choosing the forum) has a Job Board page of its own. When no live Writing exists, session entry opens the first such proceeding itself and returns its board as `opened`, so the fast open (`/work`'s section 7) reads it from there and makes no render. A proceeding chosen later from the picker is still rendered by `proceeding_id` with `draft_dir`. When live Writings exist too, each such proceeding is also a card in the picker, titled "Proceeding <id>" with "forum not yet chosen" or "no Writing yet" as its subtitle. A session there is assigned, or outlines, only that proceeding's jobs, which never name a Writing.

**A Job Board with no Writing** (work that is not a document, on a proceeding that has a Writing) is a card in the picker too, in session entry's order, under its own title with "Job Board, no Writing" as its subtitle and no mockup status. Session entry lists it flagged `job_board_only` and never opens it first: it opens only when the practitioner names or picks it, rendered by its id with `draft_dir`. A session there is assigned, or outlines, only that board's own jobs. A closed one sits in the retired group as "Closed" with its date, and selecting it offers to reopen it, never a render.
