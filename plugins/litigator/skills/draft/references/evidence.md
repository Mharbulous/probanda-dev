## A better copy arrives

One sentence carries the whole ripple. "Here is the signed original" means: record the new copy, re-judge which copy is best on the ranked dimensions, lifecycle stage, then completeness, then quality, in strict order, never averaged, move the selection, and re-render. The practitioner never re-plumbs an index line or an exhibit reference.

A superseded copy is never deleted, and anything already assembled keeps what it attached. The stale-selection marker is a safety net, not a workflow: where a copy arrived after the selection was last judged, the mockup says so on review, before anything is assembled from it.

## Admissibility concerns

An admissibility concern is captured on the spot, in the practitioner's words, with one line of acknowledgment and no pause, the skill's own spotted concern the same way. It blocks nothing, because the practitioner may choose to run the risk.

Concerns accumulate silently and are disposed of in one consolidated pass at the filing-ready declaration, before any emitter runs, each with the same three options: keep and defend, swap the evidence, or hold in reserve.

Whether a concern survives is a question of law: it goes through the research gate, never answered from the skill's own view.

When the practitioner decides a piece of evidence is inadmissible, the decision is recorded in their words by `/drafting-data`'s inadmissible-evidence write, never proposed or worded by the skill, and a concern alone records none.

## Witness statements

Paragraph breaks and fact links are read back for confirmation before they are written. A silence in a witness's account is asked about, never filled.

Nothing is signable until something is drafted, the mockup never asks a witness to execute an empty statement.

An affidavit's sworn status and made date are stated from the court-file check, never from drafting data alone (SKILL.md prohibition 18).

The skill records rungs; the practitioner climbs them. Interviewing a witness and obtaining a signature happen in the world. The skill stores what comes back, regenerates, and shows the new state, it never invents or backfills an account.

The loop is multi-session by design: because state lives in the data and renders on the mockup, no session depends on the previous session's conversation to know where things stand.

## Admissions

A response's classification of each item is read back for confirmation, quoting the response's own words, before it is recorded. Items the response is silent on are named as such.

Whether a late answer displaces an accrued admission is a question of law and goes through the gate, the skill relays the answer rather than supplying one, and the item's state does not move meanwhile.

Practitioners do not wait on themselves. Waiting is only ever on an external party; an unwritten reply or an unsworn affidavit is our own outstanding work, never a wait.

Admitted is not yet proved. Selection into a Writing is what turns an admission from paper into proof, and only then does the line go clean. For each admission, ask which proof the practitioner wants put before the tribunal.

Service is the practitioner's act, never the skill's. The skill records that service happened as content of the Writing and re-renders. Diarizing the resulting period is the practitioner's own step in a separate session. The skill still never computes, fetches or restates how long remains, and never stores a deadline in a job, idea or handover. Nothing nags in the meantime.
