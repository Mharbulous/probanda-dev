# Unattended run

**Unattended run.** Started only by `/draft unattended <Writing title>` or by the nightly launch (`references/nightly-launch.md`). Nobody is present, so the Opening takes no answer and the run works one job at most. The mode changes two things only: the `eligible:` tag on the `live` lane of `board_card` chooses the job, and every path that would pause for the practitioner stops and reports instead. The job's recorded billing anchor and the launch itself satisfy the live-issue test, so nothing further is asked.

1. **Open and render.** Open the named Writing (section 7) and render fresh (section 11), lapse cleanup included. A title that does not name exactly one Writing on this matter stops the run, because the Writing picker needs an answer. The Opening announcements are not made: each would ask a question, add jobs or start assembly.
2. **Choose.** Take the first job in the Ready order of `board_card(lanes: ["live"])`, read after that render, whose line reads `eligible: yes`. Every Ready job ahead of it is passed over, and with no eligible job every Ready job is. For each, report every reason its `eligible: no (…)` lists, in lawyer's terms, and work out none yourself: `not_ready` not Ready; `filing_filed` the Writing is filed; `no_inventory` the board has no marker inventory; `not_one_step` not exactly one step; `no_proposal_keys` no Proposal behind it; `dead_key` its marker no longer fires; `reworded` reworded from its Proposal's fixed text, or its item's marker has moved on to a later stage; `marker_not_eligible` its marker is not one that can run with nobody present; `live_partner` a live either/or partner; `shared_material` it covers material a job in progress on any Writing in this proceeding also covers. With no eligible job, assign nothing and report.
3. **Check, then assign.** A job whose id is in `ingest_inputs.ready_jobs` (session entry's `opened`, or the Job Board document's after a later render) first takes the input check (`references/job-session.md` Plan), and anything but a clean result assigns nothing, reports and stops. Run the pre-claim check (`references/job-session.md` Plan) on the job's card (`board_card(jobs: [N])`). Assign only if the job is still eligible and the check gives its first row, or its last row after the report line. Any other result assigns nothing and reports.
4. **Dispatch.** A newly assigned one-step job is dispatched as `references/job-session.md` Plan says. A lost race (`drafting_data__assign_job` returning `lost_race`) stops and reports, never retried, and so does `before_bf`, a job held until its bring-forward date. Execute the one step as the worklist directs, stamp it and complete the job through `drafting_data__complete_job` (`/drafting-data` §6, §9), which also rewrites the Job Board, so no separate render follows it, and stop. One job per run.
5. **Stop and report** wherever an attended session would pause: a decision form, a question, an obstacle, a job in progress elsewhere, a scope expansion, a lost or uncertain session token. A stop after the assignment writes no plan, step stamp or key deletion from then on. The job stays in progress under the run's lock, and the board shows it held by a run with nobody present.

It never takes over a job. It never offers or applies the practitioner's override (`references/assemble-then-gate.md`), even when the launch text asks for it: it assembles nothing and reports each unmet drafting criterion. It never outlines, picks, shelves or restores a Proposal or a job, and never releases its own job. It never creates a job, and never creates the gate job: `/drafting-data` §14's fallback leaves that to the next attended session. Captured ideas are still captured as they surface, each naming the unattended run as what surfaced it.

**The report** is the run's final message and its only output: the dispatch line verbatim, "not re-checked since" included; every item written, in lawyer's terms; each job passed over and the reasons its `eligible:` line gives; any stop or obstacle, and where it left the job; that the gate job is never this run's and is left to the next attended session; and that the run has ended.
