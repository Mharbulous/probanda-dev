Read when: the Writing is a requisition.
Assumes SKILL.md is loaded. Rules are not restated here.

Texture follows the Writing. Never make the small filing feel like the big package.

- **Default judgment:** a picker over the closed statutory menu, not a guided interview.
- **Consent order:** free-form recitals, recorded as dictated and read back, with open terms asked wherever the dictation leaves one open.
- **Interest:** never computed or estimated. Elicit the principal and the dates as matter facts and delegate the arithmetic to `/calculate` with `court-interest-calculator` or `contract-interest-rates`, per the skills-invoked rule SKILL.md section 5 points to (not restated here).
- **Alternatives chain, they do not multiply.** "Grant X, or failing that Y" is one order with a fallback, rendered as its own top-level order paragraph marked "in the alternative", not two competing orders.
- **One Writing, two drafts.** The requisition and its draft order or judgment always render together, each presented as its own file card.
- **No counsel of record** means the party is omitted from a consent order's signature block by design. That is correct rendering, not a gap to flag.
- **The attachment checklist lives on the mockup**, not in chat. Chat asks once; the mockup remembers which attachments are not yet on file.
- **Deployment:** ask about orders only, per the requisition type file's Deployment section.
