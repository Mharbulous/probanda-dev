A research return reporting an amendment in force to a rule or form either file names runs the check again, offers a `/localize` refresh on an overlay file and puts a job on the board (SKILL.md section 14, "Forum-procedure change").
4. **Ambiguous.** Relay an `ambiguous` result, from the resolve or from `/localize`, to the practitioner as one question, naming each candidate and the rule that found it, and proceed on the one they choose. A `/localize` return is re-called with `alias_of` set to their choice, or with `new_venue: true` when no venue fits. A type that none fits takes step 5's Unstructured route.
5. **A miss.** An affidavit supporting the open Notice of Application or Application Response is not drafted as a Writing of its own: store it on that Writing through `/drafting-data`'s supporting-affidavit recipe (its filing checklist), and its mockup renders beside the Writing's. At assembly, `drafting_forms__emit_affidavit` emits its text and `/publish` with `publish-bcsc-form asmaff <document_id>`, given the host Writing's folder, fills Form 109 from it. Any other affidavit, with no host Writing `/draft` holds, and Forms 15 and 16 take the `/publish` route that follows. A `not_found` kind that `/publish`'s tree resolves or near-misses goes to `/publish`. Any other `not_found` kind is drafted as an Unstructured Writing, with no `/localize` at creation: say in one line `No type file for <phrase> at <venue>: drafting it as an Unstructured Writing; a conversion job is offered after it is sent.` (under `_NoVenue`, without ` at <venue>`), then create the Writing at once on the stock kind, the venue's `unstructured` or under `_NoVenue` `unstructured-<recipient>` for the recipient passed in step 2, the phrase as given being its title, and draft it by `references/unstructured.md`. A `near_miss` kind is disambiguated first, through `/localize` as below and step 4, and only "none fits" takes this route. Every `near_miss` is learned by `/localize` with `operation: create`, naming the calling skill `draft`, the country, jurisdiction, venue as named (or `_NoVenue`), the document type as named, the recipient and the live matter. On `created`, show the learned-file notice and resolve again. A `host_not_allowed` refusal is one question, whether to allow that host for that page, re-called with `allow_host` on yes. A `fetch_failed` refusal is one question, asking the practitioner to save the page into the firm's `_practice\knowledge\_inbox\` folder, as a PDF where possible, re-called with `saved_copy`. Say any other refusal or `no_official_source` in one line and stop. The source check's `no_sources` refusal is not one of these: section 3 governs it, and drafting goes on.
6. **No fallback.** A forum missing from the index is a miss, never a `_NoVenue` Writing.
