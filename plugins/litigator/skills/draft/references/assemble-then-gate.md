## Section 13: Assemble, then gate

When every completion criterion printed above counsel's review holds, run the noting-up sweep, the overstatement review (`references/overstatement.md`) and the consolidated evidence-concerns pass. Counsel's review follows them (section 14, `references/counsel-review.md`). The passes' findings go to the Work Board, never to a file or a card (`references/overstatement.md`, "Findings"). When the drafting conditions all hold, counsel's review included, which is the filing-ready declaration, follow `references/court-ready-assembly.md` for a kind whose type file says it is assembled there. Section 14's Opening announces both. On a kind with no stock list, whose completion criteria read pending, the practitioner's own declaration that the Writing is ready stays the trigger for both, passes first. Hand the results over as file cards (`shared/present-files.md`), but not yet as final, `references/pre-filing-gate.md` runs next for every kind that needs forum parts and is assembled, once a package exists as files. A venue's `letter` is not assembled and takes the pre-send hold instead.

**The practitioner's override.** The signature, and the decision to sign a filing whose drafting conditions are not all met, are the practitioner's. When the practitioner asks for a court-ready Word document or PDF of a kind assembled by `references/court-ready-assembly.md` while a drafting criterion is unmet, publish it now. Their request is the override: ask nothing before the file exists, and never refuse. The document's NOT FINAL cover page carries the unmet criteria, with their counts, and every gap. Never apply the override from the session's inference, from a deadline or time pressure, or for a document nobody asked for. Then:

1. Quote their request verbatim in the reply, as the record of the override, in their words, with no softening, and pass the same words to the emitter as a cover note, so the cover records them too.
2. Follow `references/court-ready-assembly.md` for the kind. The pre-review passes and the evidence-concerns pass do not run, because the override is not the filing-ready declaration. Missing data never stops the run: each gap prints as a placeholder and is listed on the cover. Only an integrity refusal stops it, as that file says.
3. Present the result as a file card (`shared/present-files.md`) with one line saying it is not final, then each warning the run returned, one line each. Where an unmet item is one the plugin lets nobody waive, add one line saying the practitioner may name it to suppress, each item then taken as its own decision (below).

The override writes nothing to the board: it marks no paragraph reviewed and no criterion or step met, and counsel's review stays the practitioner's own job. It does not open the pre-filing gate or skip it. Until every drafting criterion holds and every gate step is closed, the filing is never called ready to file, never presented as final and never recorded as filed (`references/pre-filing-gate.md`, Refusal), and "is it ready to file?" is answered with the unmet criteria and the open gate steps, never with yes. It runs in an attended session, holding a job or not, because it writes no substance. An unattended run never offers or applies it (`references/unattended-run.md`).

**Suppression, for a requirement nobody may waive.** Some requirements can be neither waived nor shelved; for now, 👤 on a fact. When the practitioner asks to waive or override one, or names one to suppress, call `skill_rules__applicable` with operation `suppress` and follow what it returns, one item at a time. A suppression is not the override: it is recorded, the requirement stands, and the item counts toward its criterion as suppressed. Once every criterion holds, the ordinary path above applies. Whenever the Writing is declared ready, presented for signature or handed over, name each suppressed item in one line with its date and counsel's words, read off the Evidence criterion's suppressed items in `board_card`, because the facts they mark still have no proof.

Every `/publish` or `/assemble` invocation from this skill passes the matter folder, the matter-data folder (section 6), and, to a package kind, the Writing folder's name, which is the name of the folder the render wrote this Writing's mockups into (the parent folder of a mockup `path` it returned). The Word and PDF deliverables go where the workspace output rule puts them: an individual document's at the top of the draft folder, under the `/file-naming` name its publishing subskill composes.

A general Writing (a letter, a memo, a release) is not assembled unless asked: once its drafting conditions all hold, `/publish` with `publish-general` makes its Word document. A letter, or an internal memo, is then sent by its `Deliver` job, and a general Writing, a venue's `letter` included, takes the pre-send hold at that Sent write in place of the pre-filing gate (`references/letters.md`).
