- **Spin off blocker jobs:** propose each blocker job's title and intent in your own visible reply, drawn from the obstacle as recorded (`shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3, propose from the record), and say where each comes from. A research blocker's intent is kept whole as a question, none in a loose thread, worded no further than the obstacle as recorded or as the practitioner put it. The session composes nothing beyond what the record supplies and never fills a silence. In the same reply say that this job will wait on them, and ask the practitioner to approve or change them, in plain text per `/show-me`. Their yes approves the proposal. A change they ask for is made, one question at a time, and read back before the write. A blocker they already worded in an earlier session (a loose thread, for example) may be proposed as worded. Before they choose, say in one sentence that on yes this job goes back to the queue unlocked, keeping its plan and every step already done, that it will show as Blocked by the new jobs, and that this session then holds no job. Their yes approves the new jobs and is also the release instruction (`references/shelve-and-restore.md`'s one exception). On yes, write by `/drafting-data` §3f, re-render, and report what §3f's confirm-read shows. The session then holds no job, dispatches nothing, and answers "what next?" as after a release (`references/dispatch.md` section 14, Dispatch). A research job is worked through `/legal-research` by whichever session is later assigned it, and this session states no law.
