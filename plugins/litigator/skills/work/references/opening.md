## Proposals

**Proposals.** The `## Proposed` and `## Shelved` lines of `board_card(lanes: ["proposed", "rejected"])` carry the Writing's marker-derived Proposals, each a `- <glyph> <title>` line over a detail line giving its key, item and lane, and the `live` lane's `🧹 lapsed keys:` line carries the lapsed keys. A Proposal in the `proposed` lane is work no queued or in-progress job has taken; one held by a job is hidden from that lane and shows as a `keys:` entry on its holder's card, or, when the holder is on a sibling Writing, as a Proposed line reading `lane: held by #N on <Writing title>`; one in `rejected`, under Shelved, was shelved. Each live job's `keys:` line carries the keys it was outlined from, each as `<key> = "<stored item>"` followed by `(now: <glyph> "<current item>")` or `(now: dead)`. The premise check reads that line: a key reading `(now: dead)` is a dead key, and a stored item that differs from its current item is a reworded one. Both are on the job's card, `board_card(jobs: [N])`, which the check reads. No line in Proposed or Shelved is a control. Proposed is not an approval step: it is the work waiting to be pulled onto the board, and it stays there until the practitioner pulls it (Intake, below). Naming one Proposal makes it a job ("A Proposal named", `references/opening-routes.md`). The page's Shelved cue says to ask a session to shelve or restore. The page draws no Proposed panel: Proposed is a `board_card` lane only.

## Lapse cleanup

**Lapse cleanup.** After `references/procedure.md` section 11's render and before the opening line, delete the shelving rows for every key on the `live` lane's `🧹 lapsed keys:` line, per `/drafting-data`'s own lapsed-row script, and say so in the fast open's closing line (`references/procedure.md` section 7). A shelving applies to the line as it read when it was shelved, so once that line's marker glyph or item text changed, the shelving no longer describes anything. Where a lapsed row carried a job, that shelved job stays in Shelved and its Proposal returns to Proposed beside it. That is designed and visible, not a defect to repair. Counsel's recorded decision that a contention needs no stated fact is not a shelving: the database resets it when the wording changes, and it never appears on the `🧹 lapsed keys` line.

## Intake

**Intake.** Intake is lazy: an Opening writes no job from Proposed by itself. With the `live` lane's `## Proposed (n)` count above 0, the Opening's only intake act is the count, which joins the fast open's closing line (`references/procedure.md` section 7 step 3) as `<n> Proposals wait in Proposed.` (`1 Proposal waits in Proposed.` for one), and nothing at all is said when it is 0. Pulling a Proposal onto the board is the practitioner's, and intake writes, by `/drafting-data`'s intake script (`/drafting-data` §3e), only when they do one of these:

- **Name a group or filter** ("all the exhibit pinpoints", "every note-up", "everything for Submission 3"): the Proposals their words pick out. Where the words fit more than one reading, ask which, and write nothing until they answer. Then render, say in one line what was added, and wait: a named batch is not a dispatch instruction.
- **Say "take the next" (or "what next?") when Ready is empty:** one job's worth, the first Proposal in the lane's printed order that intake can write and that is not the practitioner's own work (`/drafting-data` §3e), with any Proposals that join it in one job. Then Dispatch takes it (`references/dispatch.md` section 14).
- **Type a task with the invocation that names a submission:** only the Proposals keyed on that submission's own rows (`/drafting-data` §3e, "The submission's rows"), so work off that task stays in Proposed. Then the task routes as typed (`references/opening-routes.md`). A typed task that names no submission converts nothing.

One Proposal named is the fast path, not intake ("A Proposal named", `references/opening-routes.md`).

<!-- atom id=work.intake.writes ops=proposals when=unattended:false -->
What intake writes: two or more Proposals of a type in §3e's group table become one job with §3e's fixed title, one step per Proposal holding its item exactly as the card printed it; Proposals that print one title become one job holding all their keys (§3e, "One title, one job"), so no two jobs it writes share a title; every other Proposal becomes its own job. It reads `board_card(lanes: ["proposed"])` once per batch and takes only the `- <glyph> <title>` lines under the card's `## Proposed` heading whose detail line reads `lane: proposed`. It never takes a line reading `lane: held by #N on <Writing title>`, nor anything under `## Shelved`. It takes a key only when no job of any status has ever held it. A key some job once held stays in Proposed for the practitioner to name ("A Proposal named", `references/opening-routes.md`). So a job completed over a marker that still fires is not made again, a job closed as superseded leaves its Proposal for the practitioner to pick afresh, and a shelved job's Proposal that returns when its shelving lapses stays in Proposed beside it. Each job of its own is the fast path's: its title and intent are the Proposal's title exactly as the card printed it, and its billing anchor, Writing and scope follow the fast path's rules, never asked. Its steps are §6a's step derivation on its item: one step, or one per numbered or lettered part the item itself has (`/drafting-data` §3e), never its title, so the pre-claim check passes it. A note-up of a statute, regulation or rule is done provision by provision instead: its steps are the card's `step <n>:` lines, one per provision the Writing cites, and none where the card prints `steps: none`, so no step restates the title. The batch goes below every job already queued, written in the Proposed lane's printed order, so the jobs already queued stay ahead. Intake is bookkeeping, not outlining. Like the counsel's review pair, it is a fixed-text create, so the session may still be assigned a job afterwards. An intake job has no creation approval of its own: the intake is its creation, and the pre-claim check is the only check before it is assigned. When two sessions' intakes write the same job at the same moment, the session deletes both copies, says so in one line and carries on with the batch, and the next intake writes the job once. Never in an unattended run (`draft/references/unattended-run.md`). After the batch the session re-renders once and names the jobs intake added in one line.

## The ingest-coverage clause

This words an ingest coverage result: the input check's (`references/job-session.md`) or a letter's (`draft/references/letters.md`, "Ingestion check"). With `status` `unavailable`, quote its reason and never say the folders are ingested. When the reason is that Probanda does not know where the practitioner keeps each kind of document, say that in those words and offer to set it up (`/probanda-setup`). With `ok` and `to_ingest`, `damaged`, `unreadable` and `missing` all empty, name the folders checked as ingested, built from the result's `folders`, never from `ingest_inputs` alone, e.g. `Court file and discovery are ingested.` Otherwise name, in this order, the files to ingest (up to ten, by matter-relative path, then `and N more`, each worded by its entry in `records` as below), any damaged record (repair it through `/matter-data` before ingesting), any unreadable file with its error, and any missing folder. Only when a folder of a correspondence type or of client communications is among those checked does either `ok` form end with the newest ingested correspondence date, or say none carries a date; a check of court file and discovery says nothing about dates.

Each file to ingest is worded, and offered, by its entry in `records`, the same way the input check words it (`references/job-session.md`, "The input check"). One whose `possible_version_of` holds an id N, whatever its verdict, is named as a possible other version of source #N, which case data already holds: the offer for it is `/matter-data`'s `supersede-draft` on that pair, never `/ingest`, with both files' cards sent in the same turn (`/show-me`), the other file's path asked of `/matter-data`. Without `possible_version_of`, a `partial` file is named as partly ingested, a `failed` one as a file whose ingest failed, and a `none` one by its path alone, and each is offered `/ingest`.
