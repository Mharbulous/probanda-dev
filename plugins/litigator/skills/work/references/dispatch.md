## 14. Opening and scope

The Job Board is read only from `board_summary`, session entry's `opened` or a render's, and from `drafting_forms__board_card`; never the page file, never a battery of queries. The summary is short: the matter, the client, the revision, the linked to-do, and the unmet and met completion criteria. The lanes, each job as the page's collapsed card text with its dispatch, eligible and idle tags, are the card's `live` lane; the completion criteria in board order with their counts are its `criteria` lane (section 7 step 2 reads both once). A job's keys, scope and steps, the members of Completed, Shelved, Proposed and Loose Threads, and the criterion items come from `drafting_forms__board_card` (`board_card` below) too, which is read-only and reads the same inventory as the latest render. The only direct reads of job state are the gates `.claude/skills/drafting-data/SKILL.md` "Job and scrutiny operations" prescribes, each attached to a particular write. Everything else comes off the summary or a card.

<!-- atom id=work.board.clock ops=board -->
**Clock.** `locked_at`, each step's `done_at`, a render's and a `board_card` call's `rendered_at` and `bootstrapped_at` share one host local wall-clock, to the minute. "Now" for elapsed time is the latest render's `rendered_at`, or the `board_card` call's `rendered_at` when the tags were read from a card (`bootstrapped_at` before any render), never a shell or Bash `date` call.

<!-- atom id=work.board.lists ops=board -->
Job lists: Ready, In Progress, Bring Forward, Blocked, Completed, Shelved, Superseded and Loose Threads, in that order. Bring Forward holds a queued job the practitioner is holding until a day they named, each tagged `BF <date>`: the day its work may start, never a deadline, shown as a plain date with no day count. From that day the job reads as Blocked or Ready by itself. Bring Forward waits on a date and Blocked on another job; a job held by both shows in Bring Forward. Loose Threads holds the captured ideas never outlined, which only the outlining branch raises. On a Writing's Job Board page it shows their count and a plain-text cue to ask a session for the Loose Threads view page; a pre-filing proceeding's page lists them, and so does the page of a Job Board with no Writing, which shows only its own jobs and loose threads and has no Proposed lane and no completion criteria. Shelved takes precedence over every other Job list, so a shelved job shows in Shelved until it is restored, whatever else is true of it. A General Job (a job with no Writing) is on every live Writing's board in its proceeding, its card tagged General and its `drafting_forms__board_card` line ending ` · general`: the same job on two boards is one job, never a duplicate, so name it as a General Job, not as a second copy. A Writing Job, tied to one Writing, is on that Writing's board alone and untagged.

<!-- atom id=work.opening.proposals ops=proposals -->
**Proposals, lapse cleanup and intake.** At every attended Opening, after section 11's render and before the fast open's closing line (section 7 step 3), the session runs the lapse cleanup and reports the Proposed lane's count in that line. It writes no job from Proposed: intake is lazy, and pulls Proposals onto the board only when the practitioner names a group or filter, says "take the next" with Ready empty, or types a task naming a submission, which converts only that submission's Proposals. Before any of these, and before reading a job's `keys:` line or the Proposed and Shelved lanes, read `references/opening.md`, which holds what a Proposal is, the lapse cleanup and the intake. Neither the lapse cleanup nor the intake runs in an unattended run.

<!-- atom id=work.opening ops=open -->
**Opening.** No widget. The fast open's lines (section 7) are the whole opening: no Writing state, no folder inventory, no list of jobs or Proposals beyond the idle report and the dispatch below, no build paragraph and no prompt, because the board shows them. An instruction the practitioner typed with the invocation is their answer, routed below. With none (a bare `/draft`, or one naming only the matter or the Writing), the session dispatches a Ready job itself (**Dispatch**, below). When nothing was dispatched, wait, with no line inviting a reply. The reply routes below. The practitioner answers in their own words. A close-out word (shelve, release, retire, drop) routes by the object it names. Ask which if unclear:

<!-- atom id=work.opening.routes ops=route_reply -->
When the practitioner typed an instruction with the invocation, or replies after the opening, read `references/opening-routes.md` before acting on it. It holds how each reply routes: a job named, an In Progress job with every step resolved and its close form, new work, Proposals and "A Proposal named", a Blocked job whose blocker is superseded or shelved, a shelve or restore, a job deleted, a job brought forward, a job released, the Writing finished, filed or rejected by the registry, a to-do asked for, a change of completion criteria or subject, and a different Writing.

<!-- atom id=work.opening.idle ops=idle_report when=unattended:false -->
**Idle report.** In the same turn, after section 7 step 3's line and before any Opening announcement, one line per `idle` tag, no lead-in, on the `live` lane's In Progress lines: `Job #<n>: idle <elapsed> since last recorded progress, about <p>%.`, ending `, no plan yet.` in place of `, about <p>%.` where the tag does, the elapsed and the percentage copied off the tag. Where that line also carries `idle past <N> h`, the idle line ends with that text, copied verbatim, after the percentage or `no plan yet`: `, about <p>%, idle past <N> h.` Not made in an unattended run. A job without the tag gets no idle line and no remark on whether it is idle, though a Dispatch skip line still names it. With no idle tag nothing is added. State only these facts: never say or imply whether its session is alive or has ended, and never offer a take-over.

<!-- atom id=work.opening.goal ops=open,route_reply when=unattended:false -->
**Statement of goals.** At an attended Opening, read whether the opened board (a Writing's, a Job Board with no Writing, or a pre-filing proceeding's) has a statement of goals stored, off `opened.board_summary`'s `Goal: <description> (<status>)` line (or `drafting_forms__board_card`'s `goals` lane), never the `criteria` lane or its `goal` alias. With status `none` and no instruction typed with the invocation, run `shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3b, its "Goal framing" included, after the idle report, in place of any Opening announcement, and before Dispatch and before section 7's empty-board route to outlining, so a job dispatched on this board already has its goal. Its first question ends the turn. Each read-back is the reply's only fenced code block, the statement exactly as it will be saved, followed by the one question. Every write is one `drafting_data__set_board_goal` call with `db_path`, `filing_id` (a Writing's board or a Job Board with no Writing) or `proceeding_id` (a pre-filing proceeding's board), `status` and this session's token, with no `description`. Only a bare yes to that read-back records it: `status: "confirmed"`, `statement` the block's content character for character. Any other reply, "yes, that's accurate" included, records nothing: revise on a correction, then read back again. On a decline, write the inferred statement with `status: "inferred"`, or, where the record supports no inference, `status: "declined"` with no `statement`. Quote a refusal and stop. Re-render after the write. Then carry on as at a bare Opening: the announcements, then Dispatch or outlining. An instruction typed with the invocation takes priority, and the step waits for the board's next Opening with status `none`. Once the status is anything else the step never runs again for that board, whatever later changes in the record, unless the practitioner asks ("let's set the goal"), which runs it at once in any session, holding a job or not. The goal write is always in scope. Nothing in this skill treats an inferred statement as confirmed. With status `needs_consolidation` (a merged board that holds no one goal yet: the Goal line ends `, <n> statements to combine`), run the same step once, at the board's next attended Opening, but read the statements to combine off `drafting_forms__board_card`'s `goals` lane (`### To combine <n>`) and propose one combined goal from them, framed per "Goal framing", as the first read-back. The same `drafting_data__set_board_goal` write, with no `merged_index`, ends the status, whatever the practitioner answers, so the step never asks twice.

<!-- atom id=work.opening.announce ops=announce -->
**Opening announcements.** Three conditions are read off the `criteria` lane of the card section 7 step 2 read, its head line and its criterion lines in board order (`- <state> <id>: <text>`, state and counts only) and nothing else, where the drafting criteria are the lines above the `gate` line. Each is one line after the fast open's lines. The practitioner can redirect it, and an instruction they already gave takes priority.

When those lines show every criterion above `counsel-review` met, or every gate step closed, read `draft/references/opening-announcements.md`, which holds the three announcements (the pre-review passes, the declaration, a stale gate) and what each one runs.

<!-- atom id=work.dispatch ops=dispatch -->
**Dispatch.** A session with no job chooses one Ready job itself, assigns it and dispatches it, without asking the practitioner to pick. It runs at an Opening given no instruction (above), after the idle report, unless an Opening announcement proceeded or asked in that turn, and on "what next?" in a session with no job. A session is assigned at most one job, unless the practitioner asks it to work several: then it repeats Choose and Assign on a re-rendered board until it holds three, minting each job's own token with `drafting_forms__bootstrap` `{db_path}` and passing that token, never another job's, wherever a rule names this session's token. It spawns every agent the jobs need itself and works them side by side. Creating jobs does not use it up, but a session that created jobs (outlining, `/drafting-data` §3) may be assigned only one of the jobs it created in this session, and only when the practitioner names it: never a job that was on the board before, and never one another session created. Such a session never dispatches. Its "what next?" is one line saying it can take one of the jobs it created (by number) when named, and that a new `/draft` session takes any other Ready job. The instruction to work on the loose threads names the job it creates, so the session that wrote that job is assigned it in the same turn (`references/loose-thread-triage.md`, "Working the threads without a pick"). A session that only outlined a job afresh, or has completed, released or shelved its job, takes no job. It never dispatches either, but may still run an on-the-fly revision or reorder, which takes no job: its "what next?" is one line saying a new `/draft` session takes the next Ready job. `drafting_data__assign_job` refuses a job the session did not create with `not_created_here`: say the rule in one line. Intake is not outlining (above). The unattended run chooses by its own step 2 instead.

<!-- atom id=work.dispatch.choose ops=dispatch -->
1. **Choose.** Walk Ready in the board's own order, `priority` descending then job number. Never reorder it: a job is only ever skipped. Blockers, dependencies, an in-progress either/or partner and a bring-forward date not yet reached need no check, because the board already holds such a job out of Ready. A Bring Forward job is never chosen, never offered, and its date is never offered for change. Skip a job for any reason below, one line per skip. The first job not skipped is the choice. The `live` lane performs this walk: each Ready job's head line carries `dispatch: ok` or `dispatch: skip (...)` with its reasons from the list below, and the Ready lane's closing `dispatch choice:` line names the choice, so read both off the `live` lane, write one skip line per `skip` tag, and never compare `scope:` lines by hand. `eligible:` is the unattended run's verdict (`draft/references/unattended-run.md`), never a skip reason. With none, say so in one line and assign nothing. The Dispatch an Opening runs by itself never pulls a Proposal onto the board. Only the practitioner's own "take the next" or "what next?" with Ready empty does: intake writes one job's worth (`references/opening.md`, Intake), and the walk runs again on the re-rendered board. When intake finds nothing it can write, say so in one line. When the `live` lane's `## Loose Threads (n)` count is 20 or more, add one line offering to rank them on the Loose Threads page (`references/loose-thread-triage.md`); otherwise wait with no prompt.
<!-- atom id=work.dispatch.skips ops=skip_reasons -->
   - **The practitioner's own.** `/drafting-data` names the practitioner as its mover (`Counsel's review of …`). It stays in Ready for them to name.
   - **An open either/or.** Its `either-or:` names a queued partner that is not shelved (`#N (queued)`). Which side runs is the practitioner's call, so both stay in Ready for them to name.
   - **Shared material.** Its `scope:` shares an entry with an in-progress job's `scope:`, where an entry naming a kind of material without a particular item covers every item of that kind. The line names that job and the shared material, nothing more. While counsel's review is in progress, its scope covers all of the Writing's substance, so every job writing substance is skipped: nothing is edited under counsel's review.
   - **Before the gate.** Its title starts `File and serve`, `Serve the application` or `Serve the response` while the `gate` criterion is unmet. Nothing is filed or served before the pre-filing checks are passed or waived.
<!-- atom id=work.dispatch.assign ops=dispatch -->
2. **Assign and dispatch** by `references/job-session.md` Branch A. The idle report, skip lines and pre-claim line precede the assign call, and the dispatch line after it quotes the intent verbatim. In hosts where text written between tool calls is summarized rather than shown to the practitioner (Cowork), send those lines through the host's direct user-message tool (e.g. `SendUserMessage`), still before the assign call and without waiting for a reply. A one-step job takes the pre-claim check and runs on its creation approval. Every job is announced and proceeds (`references/job-session.md`, Announce and proceed). A failed pre-claim check shows its own decision form and nothing else is dispatched. A lost race re-renders and chooses again from the fresh board, never re-running the write for the job it lost.

<!-- atom id=work.dispatch.in-progress ops=take_over -->
An In Progress job, including one with every step resolved, is never chosen and never offered: the take-over form (`references/take-over.md`) is reached only by the practitioner naming that job. The skip is not lock-guarded, since two sessions reading boards moments apart can each take a job overlapping the other's, which is why scope stays live-read gated at every write (**Scope**, section 3).

<!-- atom id=work.dispatch.unattended ops=unattended_run -->
**Unattended run.** Before an unattended run starts, read `draft/references/unattended-run.md`, which holds how one is started, its five steps, what it never does, and its report.

<!-- atom id=work.dispatch.branches ops=new_work -->
Branch procedures (assigning, announce and proceed, worklist execution, outlining interview, closing read-back, opposing points): `references/job-session.md`.

<!-- atom id=work.dispatch.shelve ops=shelve,release,restore,suppress -->
**Shelve, restore, release and suppress.** The rules map serves these rules, not `draft/references/shelve-and-restore.md`. Shelving or releasing the session's own job is applied where the session stands, because `draft/job`'s rules are on its path. If the confirm read shows the job was taken over, `rules__move` to `draft/housekeeping` for its decision form. Before applying any other shelve, restore, release, suppression or unsuppression, or a move of jobs to another board (`/drafting-data` `jobs-closeout.md` §10c), `rules__move` to `draft/housekeeping`, whose rules hold what can be shelved, suppressed or moved, where, matching, confirmation, and the re-render after applying. Once it is applied, move back with `up`.

<!-- atom id=work.dispatch.bring-forward ops=bring_forward -->
**Bring forward.** Set, change or clear a BF date only when the practitioner says so and names the date, as a calendar date or a calendar phrase counted from today. Never propose, offer or infer one, and never compute one from a document or another date. A calendar date is written as given. A phrase is resolved by `/drafting-data` §10b's read-only query on the local date, never by your own arithmetic, printed in plain text as the weekday and the date, and written only on the practitioner's bare yes. A phrase with more than one reasonable reading is shown as its two or three dates and asked, never resolved silently. A business day is Monday to Friday. The write is §10b's literal `YYYY-MM-DD`, on a queued, unshelved job, then re-render. The date is the day the work may start, never a deadline (`shared/deadline-isolation.md`): no day count, no "due", and never sent to `lawyer:docket-clerk`.

<!-- atom id=work.dispatch.tangents ops=tangent -->
When the practitioner starts a read-only turn outside the job's scope, or outside any job, read `draft/references/tangents.md`, which holds the tangent budget and what is offered on the third such turn.
