## Branch A: a job is chosen

### Assign

<!-- atom id=work.assign ops=take -->
A ready job is taken by one call, `drafting_data__take_job` `{db_path, ticket_id, session_token, matter_root}`, from session entry, after one `skill_rules__applicable` call with operation `take`, which serves this path's judgements. The tool runs every other check, assigns through `drafting_data__assign_job`'s guard and writes the started entry to `ProceduralHistory.md`. Only the input check (Plan, below) comes first, on a job that takes one, except a requested draft, which takes it after the draft is shown.

- `refused` wrote nothing; its `reason` names the rule below to apply: `needs_plan` "A job that needs a plan", `long_title` and `restates` the pickup review's rewrite, `duplicates` the duplicate check on each card returned, `keys_dead`, `some_keys_dead` and `item_changed` the pre-claim check; `not_ready` and `no_steps` are said in one line. Then call it again, with `not_duplicates` listing each job judged not the same work.
- `assigned` returns `claim_no`, which every later job write takes; `header`, the reply's first line; `card`, as `board_card(jobs: [N])` prints it, never read again; `premise`, its `state` and header line; and `history`. Hand the job off with them.
- `lost_race`, `before_bf`, `not_created_here` and `no_match` wrote nothing (below). In an unattended run any outcome but `assigned` stops the run and goes in its report.

<!-- atom id=work.premise ops=premise_check -->
### Plan

**The premise check.** A job's premise is its Proposal keys and nothing else: not the intent prose, not the steps, which restate the same keys, and not the scope entries, which are live-read gated where they are used. Right after the job is assigned and before any read-back, subtract from the assigned job's own keys every key that still derives: on the job's `keys:` line on its card, `board_card(jobs: [N])`, a key still derives when its `(now: <glyph> "<current item>")` names a Proposal, in any lane, and is dead when it reads `(now: dead)`. The card reads the inventory the Opening's render saved, so there is no second render and no second predicate pass. Read the job's `keys:` line, never board_card's Proposed lane: that lane hides a Proposal while a queued or in-progress job holds its key, and the assigned job holds its own keys by construction.

<!-- atom id=work.premise.wording ops=premise_check -->
Word the two causes differently: a dead key is "the marker no longer fires on N of M items", a reworded item is "the item now reads: <new text>". A reworded item alone is reported in the header, never treated as moved, and the work proceeds as stated.

<!-- atom id=work.premise.texts ops=premise_check -->
**Where both texts come from.** A key's item has changed when its stored text differs from its current text. The stored side is the quoted item after `<key> =` on the job's `keys:` line. The current side is the quoted item after the glyph in that key's `(now: <glyph> "<current item>")` on the same line, never read from the Proposed lane, as above. The "item now reads" report prints the current side. Never compare the job's title instead: once the Change path refreshes the stored item, the title still differs, and the check would fire at every claim.

<!-- atom id=work.input-check ops=input_check,take -->
**The input check.** It runs before the assigning write for any Ready job whose id is in session entry's `opened.ingest_inputs.ready_jobs`, or, after a later render, in the Work Board document's `ingest_inputs.ready_jobs`. A job not in that list, a proceeding-wide job or a job on a Writing whose type declares no inputs, has no input check.

- **A requested draft goes first.** When the practitioner asked for this job's draft, by naming the job or asking for the Writing it drafts, the check holds nothing and nothing about ingesting is said or asked before the draft: assign the job, work it, and run the check below once, in the turn its first mockup is shown and after it. Its result is reported in one line and offered once, as `draft/references/letters.md`, "Ingestion check", words it, and on a yes each file gets the offer below. Whatever the answer, the job stays assigned, and a counsel change the ingest makes is shown as "After the ingest" there says. A job the session chose itself, and every job in an unattended run, takes the check before the assigning write, as the rest of this list says.

- Read the folders `practice_data__folder_map` gives the types in the job's `ingest_inputs.types`, and ask `/matter-data` for the ingest coverage of exactly those folders, the folders of the correspondence types and of client communications among them counting as correspondence. With no folder map, say that Probanda does not know where the practitioner keeps each kind of document, offer to set it up (`/probanda-setup`), and assign nothing. Never read a `/matter-data` file or the database for it.
- Clean, with nothing to ingest, nothing damaged or unreadable and no folder missing: one line naming the folders checked, taken from the coverage result's `folders`, never from `ingest_inputs` alone, then go on to assign.
- Otherwise assign nothing. Say `inputs_stale: <folder> (<N> files)` for each folder with files to ingest, and name any damaged or unreadable file and any missing folder. Word each file to ingest by its entry in the coverage's `records`, and make one offer for it:
  - `possible_version_of` holds an id N, whatever the verdict: say the file may be another version of source #N, which case data already holds, and offer `/matter-data`'s `supersede-draft` on that pair. Never offer `/ingest` on it. Send both files' cards in the same turn (`/show-me`), asking `/matter-data` for the other file's path. `/draft` writes no case data: the supersession is `/matter-data`'s.
  - verdict `partial` and no `possible_version_of`: say the file is partly ingested, and offer `/ingest` on it.
  - verdict `failed` and no `possible_version_of`: say an ingest of the file failed, and offer `/ingest` on it.
  - verdict `none` and no `possible_version_of`: the file has no record. Offer `/ingest` on it.
- On a yes, run what was offered, check again, then assign. A file still not held after that keeps the job unassigned. On a no, assign nothing and wait: the job stays in Ready. A `/matter-data` error is quoted in one line and nothing is assigned: never say the folders are ingested when the check could not run.
- In an unattended run (`references/dispatch.md` section 14), anything but a clean result goes in the run's report and the run stops.

<!-- atom id=work.pickup-review ops=take,pre_claim -->
**The pickup review.** A job's description is its checklist: its steps are what it says it will do, read together with its title. Before the first `take_job` call on any ready job, one-step or many-step, read its card, `board_card(jobs: [N])`, and review both:

- **Title.** Two parts, in this order:
  - (a) **Spelling cleanup**, by `drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3's rule: spelling, typos and capitalisation only, no word added, removed or reordered beyond correcting the misspelled word.
  - (b) **Shortening.** A short summary is one phrase naming the work, holding no file name, estimate, list of tasks or second clause (`take_job` refuses a longer one with `long_title`). A title that is not becomes the shortest unbroken run of the card's title, intent or one step that names the work. Keep the old title when any of its words are not in the intent, so nothing the practitioner wrote is lost.
- **Checklist.** An item is atomic when splitting it further would be ridiculous: one action on one thing. An item that joins actions or objects (by "and", "then", a comma or a semicolon) is split along those joins, each new item an unbroken run of that item's own words, in order. A split never adds an action, position, figure, statement of law or research question the intent does not state.

Write the new title and steps with no pause by `drafting_data__rewrite_job` (`drafting-data/references/jobs-run.md` §5's pickup rewrite), then call `take_job`, whose `header` prints the new title. Where the review needs a word the card does not hold, the session supplies it from the record, adding no substance and needing no adoption (`shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3). Announce and proceed (below) names what changed. An unattended run never rewrites: it takes only a job holding keys (`draft/references/unattended-run.md`, Choose), which the review skips.

The review skips a job with any step done or its plan already approved (a released job keeps its plan and stamps, as a taken-over or restored one does), a job carrying keys (its title is its Proposal's label, the fast-path test reads that title, and note-up and grouped intake jobs keep intake's steps, `draft.note-up` below), and a fixed-text job (stock, counsel's review, scrutiny review, case brief, pre-filing gate), whose titles readers match by text. It never splits or rewrites the plan marker (below, "A job that needs a plan").

**Title spelling cleanup on a held job.** Part (a) also runs on every job this session comes to hold with its plan inherited: immediately after `assigned` on a take-over, a restore or a re-taken released job, before the Job header (SKILL.md) and the announcement. It is the first act on the job. It writes the title only, by `drafting-data/references/jobs-run.md` §5's held-job title cleanup, then reads the card again so the Job header prints the corrected title, and the announcement adds `Retitled from: "<old title>"`. It never touches a step, done or not. It skips a fixed-text job (the list above), whose title stays byte-identical. It is clerical and needs no approval, so an unattended run applies it too. Part (b) stays Ready-only: a held job's title is never shortened by this review.

<!-- atom id=work.duplicate-check ops=take,pre_claim,take_over -->
**The duplicate check.** Two jobs can name the same work in different words. On a ready job, `take_job` refuses with `duplicates` and the card of each live job on the same Writing that overlaps it. A job this session comes to hold with its plan inherited (a take-over, a restore or a re-taken released job) takes the check after its title cleanup and before the announcement: compare it with every other live job on its board (Ready, Blocked or In Progress, unshelved and not deleted) on the same Writing, so a General Job compares only with General Jobs, reading the titles off `board_card(lanes: ["live"])`, then `board_card(jobs: [...])` for each one whose title, form, scope or keys overlap the job's. One is a likely duplicate when its intent and steps name the same work on the same thing. A completed job is never compared (a form is one job, Branch B), and a fixed-text job is skipped on either side (the list the pickup review skips).

- **None found:** say nothing and go on, passing each card judged not the same work in `take_job`'s `not_duplicates`. The check adds no line and no pause.
- **A likely duplicate that can be merged** (Ready, or the job this session holds): say in one line that Job #M may be the same work as Job #N, and why. Then one decision form. Propose a survivor: the job holding a done step or held by this session, otherwise the job whose steps should run first. Show the combined steps in the order the merge leaves them, the survivor's first, then the other job's steps not yet done. Offer yes, change (the other job survives instead) or no (both stay, and the job goes on). A merge is a one-way-door act and needs the practitioner's bare yes, which a hook checks. On yes, merge with `drafting_data__merge_jobs` (`drafting-data/references/jobs-closeout.md` §9c), then re-render. A Ready survivor then takes the pickup review and `take_job` afresh, and a held one the take-over announcement (`references/take-over.md`). On no, nothing is written and the job goes on.
- **A likely duplicate that cannot be merged** (in progress in another session, Blocked, or with an either/or partner): name it in one line, then ask whether to go ahead with this job or leave it in Ready. Never merge it, and never touch a job another session holds.
- **A refusal** from `merge_jobs` wrote nothing: quote its note in one line, re-render, and ask the same question as the line above.

In an unattended run (`references/dispatch.md` section 14) nothing is shown and nothing waits: a likely duplicate assigns nothing, the run reports both job numbers and why, and stops. It never merges.

<!-- atom id=work.pre-claim ops=pre_claim,take -->
**The pre-claim check.** For a ready one-step job the premise check runs before the assigning write, not after it, because once assigned a job whose work has gone is this session's own, which it may not close, and letting go of it would end the session's job work for nothing. `take_job` runs it on the job's keys. Practitioner-only work also fails it: assign nothing, say which, wait. What each result does:

| Result | What the session does |
|---|---|
| `assigned`, its premise line saying each marker still fires or the item now reads otherwise, or no keys | The premise line, then dispatch. Still no pause. |
| `keys_dead` | Say the marker no longer fires, then one decision form: close it as superseded, or keep it and say what it should now do. |
| `some_keys_dead` (a hand-merged one-step job) | The same form without the close option. |
| `item_changed`, on a fast-path job (one holding a key whose title and intent are the same text) | No silent dispatch. Show both texts and offer three choices: run it as worded, close it as superseded, or re-outline it. |

<!-- atom id=work.pre-claim.outcomes ops=pre_claim,take -->
Closing as superseded is bookkeeping by `drafting-data/references/jobs-closeout.md` §9b, then a re-render. Keeping it or re-outlining it is an outline afresh of that job (Branch B, "A Blocked job whose blocker is superseded or shelved"), whose write drops the dead keys, and the session then assigns nothing. Running it as worded assigns it with `drafting_data__assign_job` and dispatches the recorded intent unchanged. A taken-over job gets the read-only report in `references/take-over.md` instead.

<!-- atom id=work.pre-claim.unattended ops=pre_claim,take when=unattended:true -->
In an unattended run (`references/dispatch.md` section 14) nothing is shown and nothing waits: `assigned` dispatches, and every refusal is reported, with both texts where an item changed, and the run stops.

<!-- atom id=work.posture-check ops=pre_claim,plan_readback,take -->
**The posture check.** When the job will write or revise a passage that rests on a procedural step, `draft/references/posture-check.md` runs here: on a job of two or more steps after the assigning write and before the read-back, its block joining the read-back, and on a one-step job with the pre-claim check above, which a flagged direction fails. It is part of the single plan-approval gate, never a second pause.

<!-- atom id=work.premise.never-holds ops=premise_check,pre_claim -->
**Never say the premise holds.** Say each key's marker still fires, which is the whole of what the check knows: it inherits every marker defect the renderer has. Never report a shift of glyph within a key that still derives, because the conversion record stores no marker snapshot and which glyph stood at outlining is unknowable.

<!-- atom id=work.steps.list ops=change,new_work -->
**Steps given as a list.** When the practitioner gives steps as a numbered or bulleted list, each item is one step, its number or bullet removed and its words unchanged.

<!-- atom id=work.steps.restated ops=take,pre_claim,plan_readback,take_over -->
**A step that only restates the request.** The checklist is the job's plan: each step says how the work gets done, not what the job is. A step that equals or contains the title or the intent names no work: it restates the request. On a ready job `take_job` refuses it with `restates`, naming the step. On a job this session already holds, compare each step not yet done with the job's title and with its intent, ignoring case, surrounding and repeated whitespace, trailing punctuation, and any spelling the title's cleanup corrected (`drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3), before any announcement of steps in this Branch. The check skips a fixed-text job (the list the pickup review skips), whose steps are fixed text. Never announce such a step as a step to run, never dispatch on it, and never ask whether it is still right. Assign nothing and write nothing until it is rewritten. In your visible reply say that step N only restates the request and so names no work, then rewrite the step division from the job's recorded words, with no adoption ceremony (`shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3). While any such step stands, the job is neither assigned nor proceeds. On a ready job the rewrite is `drafting_data__rewrite_job`, then `take_job` again, and on a job already this session's it is §5's partial replace, named in the announcement. On a taken-over or restored job, say before rewriting that the inherited stamps will be discarded. A step already done is shown as done and is not checked. In an unattended run (`references/dispatch.md` section 14) nothing is shown and nothing waits: the job is not assigned, and the run reports which step restates the request and stops. Once no step restates the request, compare the plan with `shared/job-planning.md` and upgrade one that falls short by its section 3, or, in an unattended run, report and stop as it says.

**A job that needs a plan.** A job whose only step is the plan marker, `Create a plan for how to complete this job.` character for character (`drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3, "The plan marker"), has no plan yet: `take_job` refuses it with `needs_plan`, the restating check never flags it, and the pre-claim check never takes it as a one-step job's plan. The pickup review leaves that step alone. Never announce it as a step to run or as an approved plan, never dispatch on it, never stamp it done, and never assign the job or stamp its approval while the marker is its only step. Say in one line that Job N needs a plan, then run `/job-planning` on it. On a ready job its pickup rewrite replaces the marker with the planned steps, and the job then takes the pickup review and `take_job` afresh on them. On a job this session already holds, its partial replace replaces the marker before any announcement. In an unattended run nothing is written: the job is not assigned, and the run reports that it needs a plan and stops.

<!-- atom id=work.outline ops=new_work -->
## Branch B: new work (outlining)

Records jobs, dependencies, blockers, conflicts, priorities. Never substance: no proceeding or Writing row either (section 7). The practitioner supplies everything the record cannot; the session asks, records and reads back. Where the record holds the candidates, it proposes them per `shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3. It proposes nothing the record cannot supply and never fills a silence. It never asks for the board's statement of goals: that is the Opening's (`references/dispatch.md` section 14, "Statement of goals"), asked once per board.

<!-- atom id=work.outline.start ops=new_work -->
- **An empty board:** say so and ask what work the application needs. Ask who to interview, what to locate, what to ask the client, and their theory of the case. Offer no candidates. Where the board carries Proposals but no job and no loose thread, the routing here is unchanged but the wording is not: say the board has no jobs yet, never that it is empty, because it still carries Proposals. On a Work Board with no Writing, ask what work the board is for in place of what the application needs, and write each job to that board, never proceeding-wide.
- Markers, and any problem the practitioner reports, may be read into and shown. What to do is proposed per the posture only where the record holds the candidates, and a gap the record cannot fill is asked empty-handed. A fix for a problem the practitioner reports is never proposed (SKILL.md section 2). The mockup already shows those same markers, so re-reading them into chat adds nothing the practitioner is not looking at.
- Ask what they have in mind, and raise each captured idea (`references/loose-thread-triage.md` Step 1's query).
- After that opening question, before any read-back, run the currency check of `shared/procedural-history.md` on the matter's `ProceduralHistory.md`. Where the outlining needs the history, read the whole file. Never ask the practitioner to recount what happened: the check's one question is the only question about the history. A past event read from the file is content.

<!-- atom id=work.outline.pick ops=new_work -->
**A Proposal picked.** The Proposed lane is never pointed at as a starting menu: no Proposal is offered, named first or ranked, and the case-context questions above come first whatever that lane shows, except for an unedited pick named at the Opening, which is written first and never raised at the closing read-back. A job intake wrote in this session (`references/dispatch.md` section 14) is already on the board and is not raised there either. A pick is received whenever the practitioner makes one, and read off `board_card(lanes: ["proposed"])`. On the first, say in one sentence that each Proposal picked unedited becomes its own job, and that naming several in words as one piece of work merges them instead. What follows turns on whether they changed its fixed label.

<!-- atom id=work.outline.pick-paths ops=new_work -->
- **Left unedited:** the pick is the approval. Write a job whose title and intent are the Proposal's title as the card printed it, character for character, and whose steps are §6a's step derivation on that item (`drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3, the fast path), so that each step names the work rather than restating the title, and the pre-claim check passes it. No read-back, no approve step, no question of any kind. Several unedited picks are several such jobs, one each: the session never folds picks together, the interview's "how the work divides into jobs and steps is the session's own call" is no licence to.
- **Its label changed:** the fast path is off, because nothing fixed is left to fill the intent and the step. Their edited words are an answer like any other, and the job is outlined through the interview below and confirmed at the closing read-back. The title is cut from the edited text. The intent is one sentence of theirs, the edited text itself where that is a sentence, otherwise asked. Never compose one out of the edited words and the Proposal's text.
- **Several named as one piece of work:** a merge, and only on their own words saying so. It is outlined through the interview: its title is worded from the answer naming the work by `drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3's rule, never one of the Proposals' labels, and where there is no such answer it is a gap put as a question, as at the closing read-back. The merged job records every key it was outlined from.
- **A picked key already held or shelved:** read each picked key's lane off `board_card(key: <key>)` when the pick is received: a key the card returns as a live job, or as a Proposed line reading `lane: held by #N …`, is held; a key it returns under Shelved is shelved. A held or shelved key gets no job and no conversion record, however it was picked, and its work is not outlined here. Name each holder in one line, and each shelved key as shelved and restorable on request. A shelved key is never restored implicitly: restoring is the practitioner's own request (`references/dispatch.md` section 14, "Shelve and restore"), and a key restored after its pick was refused is picked afresh. Picked keys still in Proposed carry on down the path their pick took, and a merge that lost a key asks afresh what the remaining work is called and what it is. Nothing is taken over or assigned, and the interview runs until the practitioner ends it. A holder they ask for is named at the Opening, restated after the closing read-back, so the answers gathered so far are recorded first.

<!-- atom id=work.outline.pick-record ops=new_work -->
A job outlined from a Proposal, on any of those three paths, carries the conversion record and takes its Writing, scope and billing anchor by `/drafting-data` (`drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3).

<!-- atom id=work.outline.shelved ops=new_work,shelve -->
**A Proposal shelved** in the interview is applied when they say it, as the bookkeeping `/drafting-data` specifies, and is not written as a job. It is neither outlining nor a tangent. Shelving is received, never offered: no Proposal is put up for it, and the three-way choice offered for a captured idea is not offered here.

<!-- atom id=work.outline.interview ops=new_work,change -->
**The outlining interview** (Branch B, and Branch A's change path): one question at a time, answers recorded in their words, closing read-back confirmed before any write. Only the practitioner ends it, by saying there is nothing further, never infer the end. How the work divides into jobs and steps, and what waits on what, is the session's own call and is never asked.

- The application: what is sought, against whom, on what basis, what evidence is in hand, what the other side will say, what must be in place before it is heard. Where scope is open, confirm it back in their terms.
- Which Writing or proceeding the work belongs to is data: the open board's, unless another Writing, forum or proceeding is named, then asked; a new one goes to section 8.
- Gaps as questions: where a figure comes from, what the court will be told, what evidence exists. Never the content.
- Order: only "which of these cannot both happen?" Never ask which job comes first, and never assert a priority. Jobs that depend on each other take their order from the dependency, and the rest are worked in parallel. An order the practitioner volunteers is recorded.
- Legal views: record as their proposition and ask whether they want it verified as a research job (`drafting-data/references/jobs-stock.md` §3l) before anything depends on it. Ask that of each proposition before the read-back. No structure or order decision stands in for it. Never dispatch unasked, never treat it as settled, never write the session's own legal impression.
- Tension: name both, ask which stands.
- Captured ideas: when the practitioner asks about them, read them by `loose_threads__read` with `mode: "triage"`, and quote each `idea` as it returns it, but with internal ids and terms put in a lawyer's words (if unable, say so and ask what it refers to), and offer a three-way choice, outline it now, leave it, or drop it, with no recommendation among the three. Outlining consumes the idea, leaving touches nothing, dropping discards it (a struck line that can be restored). With a large backlog the practitioner may take the triage route instead (`references/loose-thread-triage.md`), which ranks every open thread on the Loose Threads page; inside it the practitioner's word still decides whether each thread is outlined, left or dropped.

<!-- atom id=work.outline.form-job ops=new_work,change -->
**A form is one job.** Filling or editing a Writing that is a form is one job, whose steps are the form's parts, one step per part (a BCSC form's Part 1 to Part 4). How the work divides is otherwise the session's call, but never into one job per part. Before outlining a part of a form as a job, read that Writing's live jobs (`status IN ('queued', 'wip')`, unshelved, not deleted, on that Writing):

- **A queued job fills that form:** the part is added to it as a further step, never written as another job. It is an outline afresh of that job, carded "changes to Job #N" at the closing read-back and written by `drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3a's "Adding a step", which clears its plan approval, so the plan is approved afresh when the job is next assigned.
- **This session holds that form's job:** the part is a Change to it (Branch A's change path), added as a step and re-approved, never a captured idea or a new job.
- **Another session holds it:** name Job #N in one line and say that its session adds the part through its own Change. Write nothing.
- **More than one such job:** ask which one the part joins.
- **No live job fills that form, and the only one that did is completed (or there is none):** write one new job whose steps are every remaining part the practitioner asks for, never one job per part. A later request for another part of that form extends that job by the rules above.

A completed job is never reopened, deleted or merged for this: it stays on the board as it was.

<!-- atom id=work.blocked-job ops=new_work -->
**A Blocked job whose blocker is superseded or shelved** is raised beside loose threads. State from the record what it was waiting on and what happened to that — a blocker superseded by a retirement has no superseding job, so what happened is the Writing's retirement. Then ask open, "How do you want to handle it?", never suggesting a new figure and never proposing what the fresh outline should say. Leaving it writes nothing. Read back the job's existing steps, off its card (`board_card(jobs: [N])`), to confirm or change rather than re-planning them, and give the same key report Branch A's premise check gives, read-only: marker state is the same kind of record fact as what the job was waiting on. Only the report carries over, never Branch A's failed path — a fresh outline is not an assigned job's Change, it leaves the conversion record alone, and what the practitioner does about a moved key is one more thing they say in the interview.

<!-- atom id=work.blocked-job.rules ops=new_work -->
- **It is outlining, and it happens in place**, as is a part added to a queued form job ("A form is one job" above). The session is never assigned the job, and never outlines one afresh inside an assigned job's session, which has the Change path instead. The only creations an assigned job's session makes are the spin-off of blocker jobs (Branch A, "An obstacle on any assigned job") and the fixed-text creates (`drafting-data/references/jobs-stock.md` §3g, §3i). Such a session outlines and consumes no loose thread. What may change, what never changes, and the write itself are `drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3a's.
- **Confirming the step list unaltered keeps the stamps on steps already done.** Any rewording, reordering, addition or removal replaces the steps and clears the approval with them. Say which of the two is happening before they choose, not after.
- **It cannot keep a stale waiting-on**, or it lands straight back in the same Job list. Ask what it waits on now, if anything. They may drop it, name something else, or restore a blocker they shelved while it was queued. A newly named blocker may not itself be superseded or shelved.

<!-- atom id=work.outline.closing ops=new_work -->
**Closing read-back.** Proposals picked unedited are already written, one job each, and get no card: never present their titles again for a second selection, which could only be answered yes. Nothing is asked about them. A research offer job (`drafting-data/references/jobs-stock.md` §3i) is already written too, and gets no card. A job whose work is a question about what the law is reads back in the form (`drafting-data/references/jobs-stock.md` §3l), and selecting it approves its wording. Put the other jobs back in their words, in dependency order and otherwise the order they were outlined in, as one multi-select widget: a card per job with its title, a one-line intent, a subtitle carrying any waiting-on or either-or relationship, card value is the title, written unchanged, never re-derived. Two more subtitles come off the job's origin: "from Proposals" for one outlined from Proposals, and "changes to Job #N" for one outlined afresh. Where a job has both an origin and a relationship to carry, the origin goes first. Title, intent and the steps written with the job are worded from the recorded answers, adding no substance and needing no adoption (`shared/paralegal-posture.md` section 3). The intent is one sentence stating what they asked, in their words where an answer states it (a stock bundle job's is `drafting-data/references/jobs-stock.md` §3b's fixed text), a question kept as a question. The steps are the plan for doing it, meeting `shared/job-planning.md`, so no step equals or contains the job's title or intent, and the intent is never written as a step, not even a one-step job's only step (Plan above, "A step that only restates the request"). Where the answers give no step that names the work, word a step division drawn from them before the read-back, and card the job with those steps. On a new job the intent and each step start with a capital and end with a full stop (`drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3). The title is a short label for the work, taking in their verb for the work where they used one, cleaned by `drafting-data/references/jobs-create.md` §3's rule. Where the instruction and its subject sit in different turns ("research it"), the subject's turn supplies the words. A job no answer gives words for is a gap: put it as a question, never as a card. A stock bundle job is carded only under `/prepare`, never as a gap: its card carries §3b's fixed title. Under `/draft`, work one covers is outlined in their words, never §3b's. Nothing preselected, submit only. The selection is the confirmation, no separate approve step. One job is not a list: with exactly one card, use the ordinary approve-or-change form instead, except that a lone stock bundle job is picked or declined, never changed. Write the selected jobs in dependency order, blocker before blocked, and drop any dependency on an unselected job, saying so. Say beforehand, in one sentence, what happens to anything left unselected: an unselected job derived from a captured idea stays a captured idea; one outlined afresh stays exactly as it was, in its Job list; one outlined from Proposals is not written, and its Proposals stay in Proposed; any other unselected job is gone at session end. Selecting nothing is valid and writes nothing. A chat reply naming jobs is a valid submission. Close by rendering, which updates the Writing's Work Board page, never with a chat summary.
