# Authority names in italics

Read when: writing any text that names a legal authority. This covers rendered writings (factums, court forms, research memos, case briefs, letters drafted through /draft) and unrendered writings (emails, replies, notes and answers given in chat).

Italicize the name of every primary authority: a case, statute, regulation or rule. Mark it `*Name*`, short forms included: "the *Act*", "the *Rules*", "the *Alpha* case".

Italicize only the name. The citation after it stays roman: `*Alpha v Beta*, 2020 BCCA 1, para 5`, `*Arbitration Act*, SBC 2020, c 2, s 7`.

A secondary source (a textbook, article, bulletin, practice guide or commentary, recorded as kind `doctrine`) stays roman, its title included.

Never add or remove italics inside a verbatim excerpt: quoted words keep the source's formatting.
