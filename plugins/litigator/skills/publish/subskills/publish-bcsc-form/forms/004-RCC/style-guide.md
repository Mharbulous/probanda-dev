# Form 4 — Response to Counterclaim

Read this guide only when the classified form is Form 4 (`RCC`).

Apply `subskills/publish-bcsc-form/forms/000-SOP/style-guide.md`. Use the confirmed responding-party names
for the form's responding-party field. Additional confirmed caption groups use
the standard repeated `AND` / name / role structure.

## Numbering

Preserve the template's distinct continuation instructions:

- Part 1 continues paragraph numbering from Division 1.
- Part 2 continues paragraph numbering from Division 2.
- Part 3 continues paragraph numbering from Part 1.

Body lines inherit the numbered-paragraph formatting of the applicable
template instruction paragraph. Leave `[mmmm d, yyyy]` unchanged.

Repository enforcement: `subskills/publish-bcsc-form/scripts/convert.py` owns the Form 4 placeholders and
`subskills/publish-bcsc-form/scripts/fill_plain.py` owns the Part markers and paragraph insertion.
