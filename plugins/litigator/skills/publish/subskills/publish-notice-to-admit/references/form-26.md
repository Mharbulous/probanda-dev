# Form 26 — Notice to Admit

Prescribed-form reference for the BC Supreme Court Civil Rules, current consolidation checked
2026-08-08 against BCLaws (B.C. Reg. 168/2009, Appendix A, Form 26; consolidation current to
2026-07-14). Recheck the official consolidation if the rules or Form 26 have changed.

Source:
`https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_04`

## Locked structure and text

```text
Form 26 (Rule 7-7 (1))

[Style of Proceeding]

NOTICE TO ADMIT

[Rule 22-3 of the Supreme Court Civil Rules applies to all forms.]

To: [party(ies)]

TAKE NOTICE that the [party(ies)], [name(s) of party(ies)], requests the [party(ies)],
[name(s) of party(ies)], to admit, for the purpose of this proceeding only, the facts set out
below and the authenticity of the documents referred to below, copies of which are attached.

AND TAKE NOTICE that, unless the court otherwise orders, if the party to whom this notice is
directed does not serve a written statement, as provided in Rule 7-7 (2) of the Supreme Court
Civil Rules, within 14 days after service of a copy of this notice on that party, then the truth
of the facts and the authenticity of the documents will be deemed to be admitted.

Date: [dd/mmm/yyyy]                 [signature line]
                                      Signature of [ ] party serving notice to admit
                                                   [ ] lawyer for party(ies) serving notice to admit
                                      [type or print name]

The facts, the admission of which is requested, are:

1  [fact]

The documents, the authenticity of which admission is requested, are:

1  [document description]
```

Substitute only the bracketed fields and repeat numbered entries as needed. The two notice
paragraphs are prescribed text: do not paraphrase them.

## Layout contract

- Put `Form 26 (Rule 7-7 (1))` above the style of proceeding.
- Keep `NOTICE TO ADMIT` centered and prominent; keep the Rule 22-3 line beneath it.
- Place the date/signature block immediately after `AND TAKE NOTICE`, not at the end.
- Use a signature-length line, not a paragraph-width border.
- Use single spacing for signature labels and ordinary readable spacing elsewhere.
- Do not add Part headings. The prescribed form uses two unlabeled numbered sections.
- Attach copies in the same order as the authenticity list.
