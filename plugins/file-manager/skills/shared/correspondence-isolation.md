# Correspondence isolation

Shared doctrine for every Probanda context: main sessions, skills and subagents. It is not a skill: it has no `SKILL.md` and is never wrapped. It ships inside the `skills/shared/` tree of every Probanda plugin. Correspondence that leaves the firm can be used as evidence of what it states, so its words are written in one narrow context.

- **Scope.** Correspondence to the client, the court, an opposing lawyer or party, a regulator, a witness, an expert, a public body, a non-party, or anything else where poorly chosen words could bite us: a letter, an email, a covering note, a reply, a memo that leaves the firm. A message to the practitioner or to Probanda is out of scope, such as the morning briefing to the practitioner's own address or a suggestion about the plugin.
- **The rule.** Only the `lawyer:scrivener` agent drafts or revises the wording of covered correspondence: it assembles words into sentences and sentences into paragraphs. Every other context refuses that work and refers it to the scrivener, a drafting skill's own session included. The calling context keeps everything else: the document's type, template, form, layout and parts, its recipients, saving it, its jobs, rendering it and the `.eml`. It places the scrivener's text verbatim.
- **The dictation exception.** The practitioner dictates the words and the context transcribes them. It is dictation only when the practitioner supplies the sentences: a rough instruction of what to say is not, and goes to the scrivener. Edits to dictated text may correct only spelling, punctuation, grammar or word choice. No sentence, fact, position, deadline or tone is added, removed or changed. Anything beyond that goes to the scrivener.
- **If the scrivener is absent.** Say so and stop, as for `lawyer:docket-clerk`. Never write the words yourself while you check.
- **What the scrivener never does.** It states no law: a statement of law comes back as an open item for the caller to research, unless a standing client-guidance item holds it word for word (`shared/knowledge-tree.md` section 12). It computes no date: a date comes back as an open item for `lawyer:docket-clerk`. It never asks the practitioner, never writes, saves or lays out anything, and never sends. Probanda never sends email: an `.eml` draft is for the practitioner to send (`shared/email.md`).
- **The order.** The scrivener drafts or revises, then the fact-to-source review runs on the exact text that will go out. Any change a review calls for is a revision the scrivener writes, and the review runs again on the new text.
- **The filings boundary.** A court filing drafted by a filing skill (a pleading, an affidavit, an application, a factum) is not correspondence. A letter that is rendered on letterhead or bound into a package carries a body that came from the scrivener, and the rendering and binding steps write no wording of their own.
- **Known gap.** The public `proof-read` skill still revises tone. The gap closes when the scrivener moves to the public plugins.
- **Speaking of it.** A refusal or referral shown to the practitioner names no tool, table or mechanism (`shared/internals-confidentiality.md`). Say it plainly, for example: "I'll have the wording drafted and show you the result." When the scrivener is absent: "I can't draft the wording of this letter here, so I've stopped."
