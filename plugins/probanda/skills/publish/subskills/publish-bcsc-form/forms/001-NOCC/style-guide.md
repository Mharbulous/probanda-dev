# Form 1 — Notice of Civil Claim

Read this guide only when the classified form is Form 1 (`NOCC`).

Apply `subskills/publish-bcsc-form/forms/000-SOP/style-guide.md`. Form 1 uses the fixed role labels
Plaintiff and Defendant; do not replace them with generic originating- and
responding-party labels.

## Parts

- Part 1 contains the material facts giving rise to the claim.
- Part 2 contains the specific relief claimed.
- Part 3 contains the legal basis for that relief.

Insert supplied body lines using the numbered-paragraph formatting already
carried by the corresponding template instruction paragraph. The template
controls numbering behavior within each Part.

## Preserved fields

Leave `[mmmm d, yyyy]` and `[date]` unchanged for completion in Word. The
optional appendix fields `[CONCISE SUMMARY]` and `[OTHER ENACTMENT]` also remain
unfilled. Populate firm, court-address, and defendant-address fields only when
the confirmed context supplies them.

Repository enforcement: `subskills/publish-bcsc-form/scripts/convert.py` owns the Form 1 placeholder
mapping and `subskills/publish-bcsc-form/scripts/fill_plain.py` owns substitution and body insertion.
