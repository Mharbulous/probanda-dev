# Arbitration Act, SBC 2020, c 2, section 21: excerpt

**General duties of arbitral tribunal.** Verbatim excerpt, keyed by `rule_ref` in the front matter, each paragraph without its label.

The VanIAC Domestic Arbitration Rules, Part A, Rule 1(b)(i), define "Act" as the *Arbitration Act*, S.B.C. 2020, c. 2 (`vaniac-domestic-arbitration-rules.md`, `R. 1(b)(i)`). Part B relies on this definition without restating it: Rules 25(d) and 25(g) turn on "the Arbitral Tribunal’s duties under section 21 of the Act", and Part B nowhere says which Act that is. Part A, Rule 1(b)(i) fills that Part B gap, and contradicts no Part B provision.
