## Context

L4801 (Merrow v. Tidewater): a client email chasing an overdue account ($24,850 per the June 30, 2026 bill) with a conditional $9,000 discount attached, sent ahead of a BC Court of Appeal stay application (to be served August 28, heard September 4, 2026). The first drafts of this email sounded AI-generated; the lawyer rewrote it in their own voice before sending.

Names, parties, matter number and dollar figures are invented. What the pair below preserves is the drafting, not the facts: the AI draft and the final differ exactly as they did in the original, so the deltas at the foot of this file remain literally true of the text above them. ## Key deltas

- Opening line reworded off casual "TL;DR" to "the short version" — plain register, not a listed tell.
- "The end of the arbitration is close — probably" → "The arbitration is nearly at an end… probably" — drops the em-dash pivot (checklist: minimal em-dashes).
- Dropped "First, the housekeeping, so the rest of this email makes sense:" and "Now the more important part." and "So here's where things stand:" — item 1, self-narration.
- Dropped "I want to fight it, and I want to make it as easy as possible for you to say yes" and the "Ian has already lost one stay application outright" aside — item 2, emotional selling; final states the opinion in one plain sentence.
- "The August 28 deadline isn't arbitrary — it's the court's timeline, not mine" replaced with the concrete consequence ("I will be left with insufficient time...") — item 3.
- Four-bullet recap of the offer (balance/discount/condition/allocation) folded into two plain sentences — item 4. Note: the $15,850/$4,000 allocation bullet was cut from the final entirely, not just reworded.
- "A stay is not the outcome I want, but it protects your position..." moved from the end to right after the arrears paragraph, reframed as "If you currently lack the funds..." — item 6; the client's own problem is addressed before the merits opinion.
- "unless you instruct me otherwise, I will contact opposing counsel and consent to a stay" gains concrete specifics (4:00pm tomorrow, Monday afternoon, phone, 2:00pm) — item 7.
- Closing exit-ramp ("If the payment timing is the problem, call me today...") deleted entirely — item 8.
- **Discrepancy, not yet resolved:** the semicolon-before-"which" flagged as fixed in item 5, and the trailing "that they seek" flagged as removed in item 9, are both still present in this final as sent. Either this final predates those two polish edits, or the tells file's "Final" quotes for items 5 and 9 are idealized rather than literal. Worth reconciling the next time either file is touched.
