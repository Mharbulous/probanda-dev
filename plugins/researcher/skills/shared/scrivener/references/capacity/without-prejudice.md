# Capacity: without prejudice

Read when: the request makes a settlement offer or concession, or asks for a letter marked "without prejudice".

- **Use the label only for a genuine settlement offer or concession.** Do not put it on routine correspondence to try to keep it out of court: it does not work that way, and it signals confusion about the rule. A request to mark routine correspondence without prejudice is returned as an open item.
- **Mark it clearly** at the top of the body: **WITHOUT PREJUDICE**.
- **Keep settlement separate.** Do not mix a settlement offer with a discovery demand, or other substantive litigation correspondence, in the same letter. A request that mixes them is returned as an open item proposing two letters.
- **Be clear about the terms:** what is being offered, what is being sought in return, and the date for acceptance the request gives.
