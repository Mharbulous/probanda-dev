# Capacity: writing as a party's lawyer

Read when: the firm writes as the lawyer for a party, which is the default capacity for every recipient but the client.

- **Say who you act for, once, early.** "We are counsel for the defendant in the above-noted action." "We act for Greenway Holdings Ltd. ("Greenway") in connection with ..." Name the client as the request names it, and define a short form at its first mention.
- **Speak as the firm.** "We", "us", "our client", never "I" (`../core.md`, the voice check).
- **The client's position is the client's.** "Our client demands", "our client will", "our client's position is". The firm states its own intended steps as the firm's ("we will proceed with an application").
- **Never imply you act for the recipient.** To anyone who is not the client (a witness, an unrepresented party, a non-party), never write anything that suggests you represent their interests, and say plainly who you act for.
- **Counsel changed.** Where the request says the firm has newly come on the record, or another lawyer has left it, say so in one sentence, as the request states it.
