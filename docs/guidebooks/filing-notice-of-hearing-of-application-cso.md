# Filing a Notice of Hearing of Application through CSO

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Author: Brahm Dorst

## Purpose

This guide sets a BC Supreme Court civil chambers application down for hearing by e-filing a Notice of Hearing of Application (Form 33.01) package through Court Services Online (CSO). It is written for a lawyer, an assistant, or an LLM agent driving the browser with a human in the loop.

- **Scope:** civil chambers applications under the amended Rule 8-1 "serve then file" procedure, in force October 1, 2026, estimated at **2 hours or less** (9:45 a.m. chambers list, date set by the applicant).
- **Not covered:** applications over 2 hours, where a registrar fixes the date first and the notice of hearing follows within 7 days of that (Rule 8-1(14.3) and (14.5)(b)), and Family Rules applications (Rule 10-6).
- **Worked example:** Duckworth v. Pathfinder Ventures Inc. et al., VLC-S-S-256622, CSO package 4426789, filed October 23, 2026.
- **Division of labour:** the agent fills each page; the human reviews it and clicks **Next**. The human signs every document. Neither the agent nor the human works out a deadline by hand; dates go to the docket clerk.

## Before you start

Do not file anything until every precondition below holds. Under the amended rule you serve first and file later, and filing the package is what sets the hearing.

**Preconditions (Rule 8-1(14.1)):**

- [ ] The notice of application and supporting affidavits have been **served** on every application respondent (Rule 8-1(3)).
- [ ] Each respondent has **served an application response**, or the time to serve one under Rule 8-1(9) has expired. Get that date from the docket clerk; do not count it by hand.
- [ ] The hearing estimate is **2 hours or less**, so under Rule 8-1(14.2) the hearing goes on the 9:45 a.m. list on a chambers day the applicant chooses.
- [ ] Filing and serving the notice can be done **at least 4 business days before the hearing** (Rule 8-1(14.5)(a)). The docket clerk confirms the date.

**What the package must contain (Rule 8-1(14.4)):** the notice of hearing of application (Form 33.01), the notice of application as served, the original of every affidavit served with it that is not already filed, and any other served material not already filed.

**Package checklist** (in the matter's `5. COURT FILE#…` folder):

| # | Document | CSO document type | Must be |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Notice of Hearing of Application (Form 33.01) | Notice of Hearing of Application | Signed by the lawyer; Part 1 shows the actual service date and whether each respondent served a response |
| 2 | Notice of Application (Form 32) | Notice of Application | The signed version that was served |
| 3 | Each affidavit not already filed | Affidavit in Support of Application | Sworn or affirmed, with exhibits stamped |
| 4 | One Electronic Filing Statement per affidavit | Electronic Filing Statement - Supreme | **Signed** by the lawyer (next section) |

An affidavit filed earlier in the proceeding is not filed again. In the worked example, Affidavit #1 of Stan Duckworth was filed in 2025, so it was left out.

**Fees (worked example):** $80.00 for the notice of application, $7.00 CSO package fee, and $0.00 for the notice of hearing, affidavits and EFSs. The total was $87.00.

## Electronic Filing Statements (Form 119)

Every affidavit filed electronically needs its own signed Form 119 statement from the lawyer (Rule 23-3(6)). Check each one before you start CSO. An unsigned EFS is the most likely thing to stop the package.

- **When:** one per affidavit, or any other signed document filed for evidentiary purposes. The notice of hearing and notice of application don't need one.
- **Paragraph 1:** Form 119 asks for the "[type and identifying description of document]" and doesn't require a date. Firm practice is to describe the affidavit by number, deponent **and date**, for example: "The First Affidavit of Monnie Chen in this case made on October 2, 2026 is being submitted for filing electronically on behalf of the Plaintiff, Stan Duckworth."
- **Paragraphs 2 and 3:** keep the form's wording on the original signature and the true copy.
- **Signature line:** make clear the lawyer is signing, for example "Signature of lawyer for the Plaintiff, Brahm Dorst". Don't let the client's name appear to be the signer.
- **Check before filing:** open each EFS PDF and look at it. Confirm there is a signature on the line, that paragraph 1 names the right affidavit, and that the file name says SIGNED. A typed text check can't see a signature.

## CSO walkthrough, page by page

Start at the e-file home page (`https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/filing/submission/efileHome.do`), signed in, and click **Add Package**. On every page: fill it in completely, review it, then the human clicks **Next**.

1. **Step 1: Identification**
    - **New or existing file:** Existing.
    - **Court/Case File Number:** the number with no registry letters, e.g. `256622`, not `S256622`. Click **Search** to open the look-up, pick the row whose style of cause is your case (the same number can exist in other registries; 256622 also matched a New Westminster file), and the location, level and class fill in.
    - **Filing information:** Vancouver Law Courts (VLC), S - Supreme, S - Supreme Civil (General).
    - **Rejected document, fee waiver, CFCSA, protection order, rush:** all No.
    - **E-mail notification:** Yes.
    - **Your file number:** client.matter, e.g. `2575.L4443`.
2. **Step 2: Package Access**
    - Add each colleague who needs the package, e.g. CHEN, MONNIE with UPDATE ACCESS, and click **Save** (the small link, not Next). Confirm the name appears under Users with Access.
3. **Step 3: Add Documents**, one document per Save, in this order: notice of hearing, notice of application, then each affidavit followed by its EFS.
    - **Document Type**, then **Choose File**, then the three questions, then **Save**. Confirm the row appears in Documents to be Submitted before adding the next.
    - **Amends a previous document:** No. **Virtual Chambers:** No.
    - **Should this document go to Scheduling?** No for a hearing of 2 hours or less. The court's Scheduling page says it cannot assist with "Chambers hearings under 2 hours"; those are registry matters. Use Yes only for a long chambers hearing a registrar has fixed.
    - If a row is opened with **Edit** by mistake, click **Cancel** to close it unchanged.
4. **Step 3B: Verify Flattened Documents** (appears when CSO flattens a PDF, typically a large scanned affidavit)
    - Click **Review** to download the flattened copy, compare it page by page with the original, then tick **I Agree**. If anything is lost, tick **Submit Original** instead.
    - What to compare: page count and sizes, the text of every page, every page's image (signatures, commissioner stamps, exhibit stamps, highlighting), and page orientation. In the worked example, 73 of 74 pages were pixel-identical, and page 74 (Exhibit J) only lost its 180° rotation setting. No information was lost.
5. **Steps 4 to 6: Parties, Request Access, Review Package**
    - Parties and restricted access are "Not Applicable" on an existing file. Check the review page against the checklist: six documents and total fees.
    - Clicking Next on Review Package **submits** the package. That click is the human's alone.
6. **Receipt page**
    - Note the package number (e.g. 4426789). Click **Print Submission Sheet** and **Print Receipt**; each downloads a PDF to Downloads (`CSOSubmissionReport_<date>.pdf`, `CSOReceipt_<date>.pdf`). Then click **Done**.

## After submission

The package is submitted, not yet filed. The submission sheet shows each document "Submitted", and the $80 notice of application fee shows "Not Processed" until the registry collects it.

- **File the receipt and submission sheet in the matter.** Run `/file-manager:housekeeping` yourself on the two downloads. It decides their names and folders and moves them only after you approve. The pattern used so far:
    - Receipt: `9. DISBURSEMENTs\<date> RCPT CSO re PKG <package> <short description> $7.00.pdf`
    - Submission sheet: `4. LAWYER BRIEF\CSO\<date> SUBSHT DorstB re <short description> S-<file no.> $<total fees>.pdf`
- **Move, never copy.** Files are moved with a native Windows move, never copied then deleted. If no move tool can reach the source folder, stop and say so. Do not leave duplicates behind.
- **Serve the filed notice.** When the registry returns the filed notice of hearing, serve a copy of the filed notice on every application respondent within the Rule 8-1(14.5)(a) time: at least 4 business days before the hearing. Record the service date.
- **Next deadlines:** the application record goes to the registry by the Rule 8-1(15) and (17) deadlines (at least 5 business days before the hearing, and no later than 4:00 p.m. two business days before it; index served two business days before). Have the docket clerk compute and record every one of these dates.

## Notes for LLM agents driving the browser

The human clicks Next; the agent does everything else on the page and stops. These lessons came from the worked example.

- **Never click Next, I Agree, Submit Original or the final submit.** Fill the whole page, report what you entered and any open question in one message, then wait for "I clicked Next".
- **Save links are fine** (Step 2 access, Step 3 each document). They add a row; they don't advance the page.
- **Pop-ups are invisible to the extension.** The Step 1 file-number look-up opens in a separate window. Open `https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/filing/lookupFileNumberInit.do?courtFileNumber=<number>` in your own tab, run the search there, pick the row by style of cause, close the tab, and type what it shows into the form.
- **Refs go stale after every Save.** The page reloads, so find the Document Type select and file input again before each document. Saving an empty form only shows "Document Type is required"; nothing is added.
- **Upload with the file-upload tool**, never by clicking Choose File (it opens a native dialog you cannot see). Stage the file from the matter folder first. Large affidavits take several seconds; wait, then confirm the new row.
- **Leave the human's own actions alone.** If they have opened an Edit, ask before you cancel it.
- **Verify, don't assume.** Look at every EFS image for a signature. For Step 3B, check every page with a deterministic pixel and text comparison first. Only pages that differ need a model's eyes. If you fan out reviewers, give each no more than 5 pages per document and split the pages evenly.
- **Dates and law:** never compute a deadline yourself (docket clerk). Answer procedural questions from the current rules and practice directions, quoted, with sources.
- **Files:** move, never copy-then-delete. Housekeeping can only be started by the human.
