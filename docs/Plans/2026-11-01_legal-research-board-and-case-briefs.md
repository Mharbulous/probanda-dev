# Legal Research board and case briefs

Status: released in Lawyer 0.17.6, Researcher 0.10.20 and Litigator 0.13.10 (approved 2026-11-01). The first build went out in 0.17.5, 0.10.19 and 0.13.9 before its review's fixes, which 0.17.6, 0.10.20 and 0.13.10 carry. Replaces the Legal Memos page (docs/Specs/2026-10-31_legal-memos-board.md) with one view page holding three tabs, and makes the case brief a structured Writing like the research memo. Terms: GLOSSARY.md, **Case brief** and **Legal memo**. Revised the same day after practitioner review, as the Revision section below records: the page now holds four tabs.

## Decisions taken

- **One page, "Legal Research"** (`Legal Research.html`), one per board holding a live Writing, beside the Milestone page. Three folder tabs as on the Arguments page, no All tab, the last tab remembered: Propositions, Case Briefs, Legal Memos.
- **Propositions** is the Raw Research view as at b5939336 (its classes, groups, search and two checkboxes), with undecided shown by default, because holding propositions not yet placed is the tab's purpose. Its counts and filters move inside the tab. Statements that belong to memo and brief Writings are left out, so the tab is not filled with research output.
- **Legal Memos** is the board built on 2026-10-31, unchanged, except that case briefs are excluded.
- **Case Briefs** lists the cases the board's Writings rely on: each case authority behind a statement of law in use on any of the board's Writings, one row per case (parallel citations folded by neutral citation). Each row shows **Briefed** (with a link to the brief file and its version), **Requested** (a live brief job names the case) or **Not briefed**. A brief saved without a job matches its case when its file name holds one of the case's citations, in any form, or by style of cause when the case or the file name holds no citation. When both hold a citation and they differ it never matches, because a second visible row is better than a false Briefed. A brief of a case no Writing relies on yet is listed below them.
- **A case brief is a structured Writing** of a new kind, with the parts Case, Facts, Procedural history, Issues, Holding, Ratio decidendi, Obiter dicta and Disposition, one numbered paragraph per item. Holding and ratio items cite the decision at a pinpoint as statements of law, as a memo's items do. It names nothing of the working matter, with no exception: a brief is always of someone else's case, read to compare with the matter, so a decision in the matter's own litigation is never briefed. The request step skips such a decision, and the brief reviewer rejects a brief of one, saying Probanda has mistaken what a case brief is. It is saved in `6. LAW`, in the decision's own folder, as `YYYY-MM-DD case brief <style of cause>, <citation>.md`. Every saved version is the same brief.
- **Requests are made only when the practitioner asks**: "brief the cases Milestone 1 relies on" creates one job per Not briefed case, and naming a case creates one. Title `Brief the case: <style of cause>, <citation>`, the case's authority in scope, fixed steps (get the decision, brief it, save it under the job). One live brief job per case per proceeding.
- **Who writes it**: holding, ratio and obiter are statements of law, so the research agent writes the brief in a new case-brief mode, and a new read-only brief reviewer checks it before it is saved: each item is the court's own voice at a pinpoint, ratio and obiter correctly told apart, and nothing of the working matter named.
- **Read paths**: propositions through `/drafting-data` as before; memos through `drafting_forms__board_card`'s `memos` lane; case briefs through a new `briefs` lane on the same tool, printed from the same reader as the tab.
- **Rename**: the next page write moves `Raw Research.html` and `Legal Memos.html`, their `- Milestone <id>` variants and their previews, to TRASH. A viewed thumbnail of either keeps its place. The html-pages hook refuses all three names.
- **Memo and brief Writings get no board of their own** (practitioner, 2026-11-01). They get no Milestone page and no view pages, and the next page write moves any they already have to TRASH. A memo or brief saved under a job is linked to that job's board and listed on its Legal Research page. One saved with no job has no board, and is listed only by the Legal Memos rule 2 and the Case Briefs citation match.
- **No schema change.** Released as patches.

## Revision after practitioner review (2026-11-01)

These override the decisions above where they differ. No schema change, released as patches.

- **Tabs**: Legal Memos, Propositions, Statutes, Cases. "Case Briefs" is now "Cases", because most listed cases have no brief. The `briefs` data key and the `briefs` lane keep their name.
- **Legal Memos sections**: Completed (not faded, because these are what can be relied on), In Progress, Shelved, Loose Threads. Loose Threads holds memos with the status `started` or `no_job`. The status itself and the `memos` lane are unchanged. A memo's section follows its recorded status, so a Westlaw report saved under a completed job is under Completed.
- **Statutes** lists each statute, regulation and rule, relied on first, with its currency, the date last checked and each recorded provision's check.
- **Cases and Statutes** open one card at a time: an open card hides the rest of the tab until it is closed or Back to all is pressed. A case has no dash in place of a version.
- **Noting-up badges** (`note-up-status.ts`). The levels the practitioner set are below. The page shows only those the database can hold:
  - A case or statute: not noted up; noted up for negative treatment only; noted up for everything. A recorded result reads as negative treatment only, because nothing records how far the check went and a badge must not claim more than the record proves.
  - A proposition on a case: not noted up; case, negative treatment only; case, everything; proposition, negative treatment only; proposition, everything. The badge is on each authority line, because one proposition can rest on several authorities.
  - A proposition on a statute: not noted up; statute level (is it in force); proposition level (has the provision been ruled ultra vires or unconstitutional).
